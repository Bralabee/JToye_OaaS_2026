# Quick Task 260830-l8g: Close #684 — a fresh volume provisions its own migrator credential

## Problem

Since the SEC-04/#552 runtime-migrator split, Flyway authenticates as `jtoye_app` with
`DB_MIGRATION_PASSWORD`, but `infra/db/init/00-create-db.sql:44` creates that role with
`DB_PASSWORD`. When the two differ (they DO in the local `.env`, by digest), a fresh
volume 28P01 crash-loops core-java with zero migrations applied (measured in #684,
2026-08-29, unblocked out-of-band by ALTER ROLE). The nightly survives only because
`e2e-nightly.yml:144` DERIVES `DB_MIGRATION_PASSWORD = DB_PASSWORD` — a workaround whose
comment names this exact init behaviour as the reason.

## Fix (D-34-10-08's close condition, plus the two coupled sites it implies)

1. **`infra/db/init/00-create-db.sql`** — create `jtoye_app` with
   `DB_MIGRATION_PASSWORD`, falling back to `DB_PASSWORD` when unset OR empty
   (single-credential setups keep working). psql mechanics: `\set migration_password ''`
   then `\getenv` (unset env leaves the var unchanged, so the pre-set empty is the
   sentinel), then `coalesce(nullif(:'migration_password',''), :'app_password')` inside
   the `format()`. Correct the stale :42-43 comment ("core-java connects as jtoye_app
   with DB_PASSWORD" — false since SEC-04; core-java connects as `jtoye_runtime`) and the
   header's same claim. `jtoye_runtime` stays on `DB_PASSWORD` — that IS the app credential.
2. **`docker-compose.full-stack.yml`** — pass `DB_MIGRATION_PASSWORD: ${DB_MIGRATION_PASSWORD:-}`
   into the postgres service environment (no `:?` guard — absence is the supported
   single-credential case), with a comment mirroring the DB_PASSWORD one.
3. **`.github/workflows/e2e-nightly.yml`** — the DERIVED comment's claim
   ("DB_MIGRATION_PASSWORD must equal DB_PASSWORD: on a fresh volume 00-create-db.sql
   creates jtoye_app AND jtoye_runtime BOTH with DB_PASSWORD") becomes FALSE with this
   fix. The derivation itself STAYS for tonight (equal credentials remain valid via the
   fallback, and the lane is #683's confirming instrument tonight — not the night to add
   a moving part); the comment is corrected to say equality is now a simplification, not
   a requirement. Splitting the nightly's credentials to exercise the split nightly is
   deliberately deferred, noted in the issue close.

## Verification

- **ARM 1 (isolated, both directions, cheap):** throwaway `postgres:15-alpine` with
  `./infra/db/init` mounted and DB_PASSWORD=pwA / DB_MIGRATION_PASSWORD=pwB (different):
  - pre-fix file: auth as `jtoye_app` with pwB **fails** 28P01 (reproduces #684), with pwA succeeds;
  - post-fix file: auth as `jtoye_app` with pwB **succeeds**, with pwA **fails**;
    `jtoye_runtime` still authenticates with pwA;
  - fallback arm: DB_MIGRATION_PASSWORD unset AND set-empty → `jtoye_app` gets pwA.
- **ARM 2 (the close condition #684 names):** full `docker compose down -v` + `up -d` on
  the real stack, whose `.env` already holds differing credentials — core-java reaches
  healthy, Flyway applies through **V64** (read numerically:
  `ORDER BY (regexp_replace(version,'\D','','g'))::int` — the TEXT-sort trap), and the
  post-cycle stack serves. Then re-seed (`seed-e2e-fixtures.sh`) and re-run
  `check-runtime-freshness` + an E2E smoke to hand the environment back whole.

## Risk note (ARM 2 is destructive by design)

`down -v` wipes postgres (dev data + Keycloak DB), MinIO, Redis, RabbitMQ volumes. All of
it re-provisions unattended — that is the property under test — and fixtures re-seed by
script. The postcode importer reloads 1,748,230 rows on first boot (minutes).
