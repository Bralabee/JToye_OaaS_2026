**Second-order consequence of the Task-1 fix, recorded because the naive reading is wrong.**
A ClusterIP load-balances per connection, and staging runs core-java at **2** replicas (production
floor 3). So after the fix each scrape reaches a possibly different pod, while `relabel_configs`
pins `instance` to the constant `core-java` — one series whose counters jump between unrelated pods,
which makes `rate()` over them unsound. This is still strictly better than today (refused connection,
no data at all, alerts firing against a healthy app) and it is the fix DEF-29-1 specifies. It is NOT
silently shipped: Task 3 records it as DEF-29-8 with what closing it would require.
