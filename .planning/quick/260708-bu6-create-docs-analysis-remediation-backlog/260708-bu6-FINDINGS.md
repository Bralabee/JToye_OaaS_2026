## Suggested sequencing (for the backlog doc)

1. **Sprint 0 — "make it actually deployable + stop the bleeding" (P0, ~1 week):** P0-1..4 are a day or two combined; P0-5 and P0-6 are the substantive items. Nothing else matters until the platform's money math and flagship compliance output are correct and the PII/secrets exposure is closed.
2. **Sprint 1 — "safe for a first real tenant" (P1, ~2 weeks):** RBAC (P1-1) and GDPR erasure (P1-2) first — they gate everything customer-facing; then the fail-closed Redis path, backups/restore drill, and CI scan gating.
3. **Sprint 2+ — "true at 3 replicas" (P2):** the built-but-not-wired batch (SSE, outbox, pools, pagination, FTS, observability, CI/CD deploy path). P2-11 (tenant lifecycle + Stripe Connect decision) is the long pole and a business decision as much as engineering.
4. **Rolling — P3** alongside feature work; P3-1/P3-2 become P1-equivalent the moment an enterprise or public-sector prospect appears.

**Bus-factor note (process, not a ticket):** 198/198 commits from one person under two git identities; no coverage gating; E2E outside CI. The backlog above reduces key-person risk only if the fixes land with tests and runbooks, not just code.
