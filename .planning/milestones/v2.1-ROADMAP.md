## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**

- **Work Orders A+B+C only** — A as 2-day standalone safety net, B and C each ~1 week. Deferred D–O to keep milestone bounded ~2.5 weeks.
- **Skip research phase** — state-of-codebase doc already research-grade with file:line evidence; phase-level research covered framework-specific pitfalls.
- **STOMP broker behind `stomp.broker.mode` flag** — keeps local dev on in-memory, staging/prod on RabbitMQ relay.
- **Phase 9 re-scope mid-flight (2026-04-15)** — `.env`-committed claim verified false; SECR-01/02/03 converted from rotation to verification; SECR-07 added for gitleaks CI enforcement. Alert destination re-scoped from Slack to email via Mailhog because no committed Slack dependency existed.
- **Phase 11 depends on Phase 9** — STMP-05 reuses Alertmanager + email route from SECR-04/SECR-05 (shared alerting infra).
- **One phase per work order** (no splitting) — task breakdown fits cleanly, preserves audit traceability.
- **ALL /topic/ subscriptions require tenant segment** (P1 deep-audit fix, PR #40) — future-proofs against new broadcast channels bypassing isolation.
- **JWT in STOMP CONNECT headers with session fallback** (P1 deep-audit fix) — backwards-compatible during rolling deploys.

**Issues Resolved:**

- Customer-facing storefront 404s on `/shop/[slug]/cart` and `/shop/orders` — both routes shipped in Phase 10.
- Storefront never showed vendor promotions/announcements — Phase 10 wired dedicated public endpoints + render.
- `core-java` couldn't scale past 1 replica without losing STOMP broadcasts — Phase 11 relay mode + verified two-replica smoke test.
- 10 Prometheus alert rules had no routing destination — Phase 9 Alertmanager wired with email receiver.
- No CI guard against future secret commits — Phase 9 gitleaks CI (SECR-07).
