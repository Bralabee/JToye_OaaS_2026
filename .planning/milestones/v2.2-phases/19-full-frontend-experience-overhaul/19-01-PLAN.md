Close the two order-creation data-integrity blockers at the backend, end-to-end (no shallow flows):

1. **UIX-03 (backlog #2):** the guest storefront order path never snapshots the product name, so `OrderItem.productName` persists its `"Unknown Product"` default and leaks onto the kitchen display + order detail. Purpose: these are AUDIT BLOCKERS that must land before any polish. Both live on `PublicStorefrontService.createGuestOrder`, so they ship together as one owner of that file.
