<objective>
Close SEC-01 by adding an application-layer tenant-match gate on every `/public/shops/{slug}/**` endpoint whose service method sets `TenantContext` from the slug. A JWT-authenticated caller for tenant A cannot slip across to tenant B by manipulating the URL slug — the service now compares the JWT-populated `TenantContext.get()` to the slug-resolved `shop.getTenantId()` BEFORE any tenant-scoped read or write, rejecting mismatches with HTTP 403 and a structured audit log.

Purpose: Closes the v2.2 SEC-01 deep-audit finding — "Application-layer tenant validation for guest tracking." Per RESEARCH.md §1-§4, there is no `GuestTrackingService`; the real threat is an authenticated-at-a-public-endpoint customer whose JWT tenant contradicts a spoofed path slug. Today `PublicStorefrontService.getShopConfig|getShopProducts|createGuestOrder` (lines 102, 214, 326) and `ReviewService.getShopReviews|createReview` (line 56 — Assumption A2 confirmed by grep) silently override `TenantContext` with the slug-derived tenant. The worst case is `createGuestOrder`, which WRITES `Order` + `OrderItem` rows with `tenant_id = shop.getTenantId()` while the actor is only authorized for a different tenant. After this plan, every one of those paths rejects cross-tenant upstream context with 403 before the override happens.

Output: (1) new `TenantAccessDeniedException extends AccessDeniedException`; (2) private `resolvePublicShopForSlug(slug)` helper in `PublicStorefrontService`, consuming 3 vulnerable call sites; (3) matching gate in `ReviewService` (2 call sites); (4) new `CrossTenantSpoofIntegrationTest` (MockMvc + Testcontainers Postgres, NOT H2, Phase 12 deviation #4) covering all 4 phase Success Criteria; (5) 4 new unit tests on `PublicStorefrontServiceTest.resolvePublicShopForSlug_*`.
</objective>
