<objective>
Expose the vendor-facing review/rejection queue as a backend surface (IMG-03 vendor-visible half + the data contract for IMG-04): a tenant-scoped GET listing FAILED (reason + re-upload) and flagged-ACTIVE (Keep/Replace) assets, a Keep action that dismisses the flag, and the product/media DTO enrichment that carries `MediaAssetStatus` + `flagged` + `failureReason` so the UI can render processing/failed/flagged states.

Purpose: A FAILED asset must surface a vendor-visible rejection reason and a flagged-ACTIVE asset must land in a review queue with Keep (dismiss) or Replace (re-upload) — without a hard "block product on FAILED" gate (D-04b). Replace reuses the 24-03 accept endpoint; Keep is a new dismiss-flag action. The DTO is the seam the 24-06 frontend renders against.

Output: `MediaController` (review-queue GET + keep POST), `MediaAssetDto` (+ the `MediaAssetStatus` mapping into product DTOs), and the two IMG-03/04 backend tests. Depends on 24-04 (worker produces FAILED/flagged) + 24-02 (model + dual-read).
</objective>
