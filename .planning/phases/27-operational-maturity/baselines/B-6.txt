--- CORRECTION TO THE REVISION BRIEF ---
The brief attributes this to DNS. It is NOT DNS: the exporter logs "Established new database
connection fingerprint=postgres:5432" immediately before failing, so the name resolved and the TCP
connection succeeded. The failure is the subsequent version query under a TLS requirement the
server cannot satisfy. 27-00-PLAN.md F-3b already carries this correction; this capture confirms it
independently against a current log window (the brief's 2026-05-05 log lines predated the run).
