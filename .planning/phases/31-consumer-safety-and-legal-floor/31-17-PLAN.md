<objective>
Make the policy pages reachable. A policy nobody can reach is not "live on the public storefront",
which is the exact wording LGL-01 is measured against.

**A correction from RESEARCH governs this plan and removes a component from the phase.** The
orchestrator brief and the UI-SPEC both stated that `/shop/[slug]` renders no `<footer>` and has no
`contentinfo` landmark, and concluded that a `StorefrontLegalStrip` was needed to supply one. That
was **measured false**: the premise about `app/shop/[slug]/layout.tsx` is correct, but Next.js nests
layouts and `app/shop/layout.tsx:73` renders `<PublicFooter />` over the entire `/shop/**` subtree.
Served HTML shows one `<footer>` on `/`, `/legal`, `/shop`, `/shop/[slug]` and
`/shop/[slug]/checkout`, and `page.getByRole("contentinfo")` resolves to 1 on every one of them.

What IS true is the second half, and it is the whole of the reachability problem: `PublicFooter` links
`/`, `/shop`, `/shop/orders`, `/track`, `/for-operators`, `/business-model-guide`, `/competitive` and
`/auth/signin` — and **no `/legal` route at all**. The only in-app link to `/legal` anywhere is
`components/platform/company-legal.tsx:20`, which renders on platform surfaces only.

So one column in one component fixes reachability on **every public route including the tenant
storefront**, and `StorefrontLegalStrip` is not built. That decision was recorded in 31-08; this plan
is where its consequence lands.

Output: the Legal column, the sitemap entries, and per-PR browser assertions that each route resolves.
</objective>
