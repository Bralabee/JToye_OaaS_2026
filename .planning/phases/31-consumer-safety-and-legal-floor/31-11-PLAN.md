<objective>
Publish the privacy notice and the cookie/browser-storage policy — the two documents LGL-01 names
that do not exist in any form today.

Purpose: `frontend/app/legal/` contains exactly one file, a Companies House trading disclosure. There
is no privacy policy. `docs/legal/article-9-allergen-basis.md` has listed *"Write the privacy notice
— there is currently none"* as its own recommended step 4 since 2026-07-30. A UK consumer-facing
storefront is required to have one.

Two things make these pages harder than prose. First, D-15's layered notice has to draw a GDPR line
(joint controllers for consumer order data) that is **different** from the trading line already drawn
on `/legal` (vendors own their own trading disclosures) without contradicting it — a regulator reads
both pages. Second, under PECR `localStorage` is storage on terminal equipment exactly as a cookie
is, so the cookie policy must be exhaustive over **eight localStorage keys, two sessionStorage keys
and four cookie families**, two of which hold an email address. A policy that says "cookies only" is
inaccurate on its face.

Output: two routes, their metadata, and tests asserting the disclosure matches the code.
</objective>
