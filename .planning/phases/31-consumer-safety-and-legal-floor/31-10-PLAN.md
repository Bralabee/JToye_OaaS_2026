<objective>
Persist the order's allergen data at write time, and expose it on the order DTO that both checkout
and the kitchen display consume.

**OPEN QUESTION RESOLVED — SNAPSHOT, NOT LIVE JOIN.** RESEARCH left this to the planner and
recommended snapshot; this plan takes that recommendation and records the reasoning as binding.
`OrderItem` today carries `id, tenantId, order, productId, productName, quantity, unitPricePennies,
totalPricePennies, createdAt` — no allergen data — so aggregation must either join back to `Product`
by `productId` at read time or snapshot at write time.

A live join means a vendor editing an allergen mask **after** the order is placed silently changes
what the customer is recorded as having acknowledged and what the kitchen ticket shows. The customer
acknowledged set A; the kitchen sees set B; no record of A exists anywhere. On the one surface in
this product that can physically injure someone, that is the wrong trade. `OrderItem` already
snapshots `productName` for exactly this class of reason — the comment at
`PublicStorefrontService.java:786-789` records that it was a root-cause fix so the kitchen display
could never show a stale default.

The cost is honest and accepted: a Flyway migration, an `order_items_aud` mirror because the entity
is `@Audited`, and `schema_version` moving in `docs/metrics.json` plus the three documents that quote
it (constraint C-11). 31-18 owns the prose updates.

Output: V63, the write-time snapshot, and the DTO fields 31-14 and 31-15 render.
</objective>
