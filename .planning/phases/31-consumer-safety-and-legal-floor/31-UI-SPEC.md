Base scale (4 sizes, 2 weights — the whole phase fits inside this):

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tailwind |
|------|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| Body | **16px** | 400 | **1.625** | `text-base leading-relaxed` |
| Label | **14px** | 600 | 1.4 | `text-sm font-semibold` |
| Heading | **20px** | 600 | **1.3** | `text-xl font-semibold` |
| Display | **28px** | 600 | **1.2** | `text-[1.75rem] leading-[1.2] font-semibold` (page `<h1>`) |

Two weights only: **400 (regular)** and **600 (semibold)**. Work Sans loads 400–900; this phase uses
two of them. `font-bold` (700) is permitted **only** where it already exists on a surface being
extended (the checkout CTA, the KDS ticket print sheet) — extending a surface is not licence to
restyle it.

**700 is barred outright from every component this phase introduces.** "Permitted" above is a
grandfather clause for those two named legacy surfaces, not a licence. No new S1–S5 component —
`CookieNotice`, the dormant consent banner, the legal/privacy/cookies/retention pages, the checkout
allergen block and its acknowledgement control, `OrderAllergenBanner`, the per-item KDS badge, or
the conformance statement — may use weight 700. New components use 400 and 600 only.

### Per-surface overrides

| Surface | Override | Reason |
|---------|----------|--------|
| **S2 legal pages** | Body is **16px at 1.625**, prose column capped at **68ch** (`max-w-[68ch]`). | `/legal` today renders its body at `text-sm` (14px) inside `max-w-3xl` (768px ≈ 90 characters) — fine for a 4-line disclosure, wrong for a multi-screen privacy notice. This is an **uplift on the new pages**; the existing `/legal` index body may stay 14px or move to 16px, but it must not shrink. |
| **S2 retention table** | Table text **14px/1.4**; header cells 14px/600. Table region may widen to `max-w-4xl`. | A 4-column table inside a 68ch column forces a scrollbar at desktop widths for no reason. |
| **S4 KDS banner** | **"ALLERGENS" label 20px/600 uppercase, tracking 0.08em** (`text-xl font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.08em]` — the base Heading size, no new step); allergen list 16px/600. Per-item badge **14px/600**. | Viewing-distance contract, below. Was 18px in the first draft: that introduced a fifth size and contradicted the 4-size scale above. Resolved **upward** to the existing 20px Heading rather than down to 16px, because this label is glanced at 0.6–1.5 m and shrinking it to fix a scale violation would trade a document-consistency problem for a legibility one. |
| **S4 item list** | Items move from the current inline 12px comma-joined `<span>` run (`app/dashboard/kitchen/page.tsx:876-884`) to a `<ul>` at **14px**, one item per row. | A 12px inline run cannot carry a per-item badge legibly, and D-04 requires staff to know *which* item. The "3 items" summary line above it **stays** — this is additive. |
| **S1 cookie notice** | Body 14px/1.5, action labels 14px/600. | It is chrome, not content; it must not compete with the page's own `<h1>`. |

### S4 viewing-distance assumptions (stated, because the contract depends on them)

The KDS is **not** read on a phone at 30 cm. The sizes above assume:

- a wall-mounted or counter-propped display, **0.6 m – 1.5 m** from the reader, or a tablet at arm's length;
- a **glanced** read under time pressure, not a studied one;
- a kitchen: glare, steam, and **gloved fingers** (which is why the 44px minimum is not negotiable here);
- the reader may be **colour-fatigued or colour-blind** — which is why no allergen signal is ever carried by colour alone (§ Accessibility).

Angular size scales as height ÷ distance, so a glyph of height `H` at 1.5 m subtends the same angle
as `H × (d ÷ 150)` at `d` cm. At the contracted **20px**, that is **8px at 60 cm** (20 × 60/150) —
legible for a short uppercase label, not for a sentence. Hence: **short uppercase label + short
list**, never prose, in the banner.

> The first draft stated "an 18px glyph subtends roughly what 6–7px does at **40 cm**". The ratio
> was mis-stated: 18 × (40/150) is **4.8px**, not 6–7px. The 6–7px figure actually corresponds to a
> ~50–60 cm reading distance, not 40 cm. Corrected here rather than carried forward, because the
> whole point of stating the assumption is that a later reviewer can check it.

## Surface Contracts

### S1 — Essential-cookies notice (D-05)

**Today's measured state:** zero non-essential cookies, zero analytics/tag scripts across
`frontend/app`, `frontend/components`, `frontend/lib`. The only browser storage is
auth/session cookies (`lib/customer-auth-cookies.ts:16-18`: `jtoye-customer-access`,
`jtoye-customer-refresh`, `jtoye-customer-id`, all `httpOnly`, `sameSite: lax`) plus `localStorage`
used for the cart, order history and shop context (`lib/cart-identity.ts`, `lib/order-history.ts`,
`lib/shop-context.ts`). **Under PECR, `localStorage` is storage on terminal equipment exactly as a
cookie is — the policy must cover it, and the notice must not claim "cookies only".**

**Therefore: what ships today is a NOTICE, not a consent gate.** The consent store ships with zero
non-essential categories registered, so no accept/reject choice is presented.

| Property | Contract |
|----------|----------|
| Type | Non-modal, dismissible notice. **Never** a modal, never an overlay that must be interacted with, never a focus trap, never a scroll lock. |
| Placement | `position: fixed`, bottom, full width, `bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`. **Out of document flow** — it must cause **zero** layout shift. |
| Surface | `bg-oxblood` with `text-cream`; a `border-t border-white/15` matching `PublicFooter`'s bottom rule. |
| Max height | ≤ 30% of a 667px-tall viewport (iPhone SE) and it must not overlap the storefront's sticky basket bar or the primary CTA at 375 × 667. |
| Controls | One button: **"Got it"** (44px min, cream fill, oxblood text) + one text link: **"Cookie policy"** → `/legal/cookies`. Both keyboard reachable, both with visible focus. |
| Dismissal persistence | `localStorage` key `jtoye-cookie-notice-ack` storing a **version string**, so a materially changed policy re-shows the notice. Dismissal itself is strictly-necessary storage (a preference the user asked for). |
| Entrance motion | 200ms fade + 8px translate-Y. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → opacity only, or instant. `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"` governs framer-motion but **not** a raw CSS transition; a CSS entrance needs its own `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block — precedent at `globals.css:147-151` (`.kds-press`). |
| Landmark | Rendered inside a `<section aria-label="Cookie notice">` — not a `<dialog>`, not `role="alertdialog"`. |
| Where it renders | Every public route, including `/shop/[slug]` (which has no footer at all today). It names J'Toye as the party storing data, which is a **PECR duty, not a Companies Act trading disclosure** — `lib/company.ts:9-12`'s rule about keeping the platform's *company identity* off tenant storefronts is not violated by this, and that distinction is recorded so it is not mis-flagged. |

**The dormant banner (contract now, visible later).** The moment any non-essential category is
registered, the notice becomes a consent banner and these apply:

- **"Reject all" must be as prominent as "Accept all"** — same size, same visual weight, same level
  in the hierarchy, adjacent. Not a text link next to a filled button.
- **No pre-ticked boxes.** Every non-essential category defaults to off.
- A third control, **"Manage cookies"**, opens per-category controls.
- Nothing in the gated category may load before a choice is recorded.
- The user can change their mind: a persistent **"Cookie settings"** link in the footer.

**Falsifiability (D-05's own warning).** A consent gate over zero categories cannot fail as shipped.
The contract is only real if a **fixture category** in tests renders the banner and demonstrates the
gate *blocking* a script and then *permitting* it after a recorded choice. **Both directions must be
captured.** A test that only shows the banner absent proves nothing.

#### S2a — the retention table (the hard case)

Four columns: **Data category · Retention period · Lawful basis · How it's enforced**.

- The fourth column is **not decoration** — it is what makes D-08's gate possible. Values are exactly
  **`Automated`** or **`Operational`**, rendered as a badge with a text label (never colour-alone).
  A row marked `Automated` must have a corresponding enforcement reference the gate can read; a row
  that is only a promise is marked `Operational` and is not gated.
- **No placeholder ever ships.** A published `TBD` in a retention schedule is a defect, not a draft.
- Markup: **one real `<table>`** with `<caption>`, `<th scope="col">` on headers and
  `<th scope="row">` on the category cell.
- **Do not restyle to `display: block` at narrow widths.** That is the well-known pattern that strips
  table semantics in several screen readers, turning a legally operative schedule into an unlabelled
  run of text.
- **Do not duplicate the data** as a second mobile-only `<dl>`. Duplicated DOM is a trap this repo has
  already paid for twice (the streaming staging buffer that put a second copy of the shell in the DOM
  and was filed as a product bug — #556, #593).
- **The contract is: it fits.** Cells wrap (no `white-space: nowrap`), the period column is short
  ("6 years", "30 days"), and the table must show **no horizontal scrollbar at 375px** —
  assertable as `scrollWidth <= clientWidth` on `/legal/retention` at a 375px viewport.
- The scroll region is the **safety net, not the plan**: the table is wrapped in
  `<div role="region" aria-label="Data retention schedule" tabindex="0" class="overflow-x-auto">`.
  The `tabindex="0"` is required — an unfocusable scroll container is exactly what axe's
  `scrollable-region-focusable` rule fails, and it makes the table unreachable by keyboard if it ever
  does overflow.
- shadcn's `Table` primitive already wraps in `<div className="relative w-full overflow-auto">`
  (`components/ui/table.tsx:9`) with **no** `role`, `aria-label` or `tabindex`. Extend it for this
  use; do not silently rely on it.

### S3 — Checkout allergen set + acknowledgement (D-02, D-03)

**The one item in this phase that can injure someone.** Precision here is not polish.

**What already exists and must be preserved** (Incremental Betterment): the live checkout renders an
"Allergen warnings" panel **after** the order is created — in the COD confirmation
(`page.tsx:423-432`) and in the payment step (`page.tsx:523-535`, with the copy *"Your order has been
created. You may proceed if you accept the allergen risk, or go back to modify your order."*). Those
panels stay. This phase adds a **pre-submit** block; it does not delete the post-creation one.

| Property | Contract |
|----------|----------|
| Position | **Step 1 of the checkout form, immediately above the "Place order" button**, after "How you'll pay". Not in the order summary, not collapsed, not behind a disclosure. It is the last thing read before committing. |
| Prominence | Full-width panel, `bg-amber-50`, `border border-amber-600` (3.19:1 — see Colour), `rounded-xl`, `p-4`. An `AlertTriangle` icon (lucide, 20px) beside the heading. |
| Heading | `<h2>` at Label size (14px/600) in amber-800, matching the surrounding `<h2>`s at `page.tsx:632`, `:696`, `:805` — the block reads as a peer of "Your details", not as chrome. |
| The allergen set | The **order's own aggregated set**, rendered as a wrapped row of chips, each chip carrying the allergen **name in words** (14px/600, amber-800 on white, `border-amber-600`). Never an icon alone, never a mask, never an abbreviation. |
| Reconciliation flag (D-03) | Visually **distinct from a declared allergen** and never colour-only: a separate line per flagged product, prefixed with an `AlertCircle` icon and the word **"Check"**, naming the product and the allergen the ingredients text mentions but the mask omits. |
| Never | The customer's stored allergen profile is **not read, not rendered, not hinted at** (D-01). No "matches your allergies", no personalised highlight. If any UI implies the platform knows the customer's allergies, the contract is violated. |
| Acknowledgement control | A **checkbox** (24px box, 44px row) with a real `<label htmlFor>`. **Not** a button, **not** a "by continuing you agree" implicit consent, **not** pre-checked. |
| Blocking behaviour | The **"Place order" button stays enabled.** Submitting without acknowledgement is refused: the form does not submit, an error is rendered in a `role="alert"` region adjacent to the checkbox, `aria-invalid="true"` is set on the checkbox, `aria-describedby` points at the error, and **focus moves to the checkbox**. |
| Why not `disabled` | The live form already disables submit for `belowMinimum` (`page.tsx:845`) — that has a permanent explanatory hint beside it. An acknowledgement gate does not: a disabled button on a touch device gives *no* feedback at all when pressed, so the user learns nothing. A refusal that announces itself is both accessible and legally stronger — the refusal is evidence the gate fired. |
| Empty set | When the order has **no** declared allergens, the panel still renders — with the honest copy below. A silently absent panel is indistinguishable from a panel that failed to render. |
| Persistence across steps | The acknowledgement is per **order intent**. If the user goes back and edits (which already rotates the idempotency key — `page.tsx:457-459`), the acknowledgement **resets**. Acknowledging one basket must not silently carry to a different one. |

**Falsifiability.** A broken control violates this contract observably: unwire the acknowledgement
state and the form submits without it (test fails); render the panel with an empty set and the
"no declared allergens" copy must appear (a blank panel fails); remove the `role="alert"` and the
refusal is silent to assistive tech (test fails). Each of these must be **run in the break direction
and the output recorded**, not merely asserted green.

#### Non-vacuity control (D-13 — binding on every axe assertion)

**A zero-violation axe result is presumed an artefact until proven otherwise.** This project has
already been bitten: a naive "0 button-name violations" was meaningless because the tables under
test never mounted.

Every axe assertion must therefore carry a **non-vacuity control that is asserted in the same test**,
proving the scanned surface actually rendered:

- **≥1 `<h1>`** and a **`main` landmark** present in the scanned tree;
- a **surface-specific content assertion with a non-zero count** — e.g. `/shop` renders ≥1 shop card;
  `/shop/[slug]` renders ≥1 product; the dish modal scan asserts `[role="dialog"]` count **= 1**
  *before* scanning; checkout asserts the order-summary line items ≥1.
- The control assertion must **fail** if the page renders empty. A control that passes on a blank
  page is not a control.
- The gate must additionally be **shown to fail against a deliberately broken control** (the
  roadmap's own wording): a fixture page or a temporarily broken component with a known violation
  (e.g. an `<img>` with no `alt`, a button with no accessible name) must produce a **non-zero
  violation count**, and that output must be recorded alongside the clean run.
- **Missing tooling, unparseable output, or an empty result set exits non-zero (VOID), never 0.**
  "Found nothing" is never "clean".

## Falsifiability Register

Every acceptance-shaped claim above, phrased so a broken control violates it. **Each must be run in
the break direction and both outputs recorded** before it is trusted (CLAUDE.md § Cross-Cutting
Quality Contracts, dimension 5a).

| # | Claim | How a broken control violates it |
|---|-------|----------------------------------|
| F1 | Consent gate blocks non-essential scripts | With a **fixture category** registered, the gated script must not load before a choice and must load after. Remove the gate → the script loads unconditionally → test fails. A zero-category assertion proves nothing and does not count. |
| F2 | Cookie notice causes no layout shift | Measure CLS on `/` with the notice shown. Make it `position: static` → shift appears → test fails. |
| F3 | Retention table fits at 375px | `scrollWidth <= clientWidth` on `/legal/retention` at 375 × 667. Add `whitespace-nowrap` → fails. |
| F4 | Every `<h2>` has a stable id and every ToC link resolves | Rename a heading without updating the anchor → dangling link → fails. |
| F5 | Checkout cannot be submitted unacknowledged | Unwire the checkbox state → submit succeeds → fails. |
| F6 | The refusal is announced | Remove `role="alert"` → the accessible-name/announcement assertion fails. |
| F7 | The customer's stored allergen profile is never rendered | Assert the checkout DOM contains no value derived from `Customer.allergenRestrictions`. Wire it in → fails. |
| F8 | KDS banner shows the complete set; badge may truncate | Order with 5 allergens: banner lists 5, badge shows 3 + "+2". Truncate the banner → fails. |
| F9 | Allergen signal is never colour-only | Remove the icon and the word from the banner → the text-content assertion fails. |
| F10 | axe = 0 violations on the declared surfaces | Introduce a known violation (an `<img>` with no `alt`) → non-zero count. Recorded alongside the clean run. |
| F11 | The axe scan was not vacuous | The dish-modal scan asserts `[role="dialog"]` count = 1 **before** scanning; `/shop` asserts ≥1 card; checkout asserts a non-empty basket. Scan an empty page → the control fails even though violations = 0. |

| F13 | Statement dates are fresh | `nextReviewDue` in the past → fails. |
| F14 | Legal pages are reachable | Assert `PublicFooter` links all five legal routes and each returns 200. Drop a link → fails. |
| F15 | Contact route on the statement resolves | A 404 or an unconfigured mailto → fails. |

**One correction landed in passing.** Re-deriving the viewing-distance claim at the new size exposed
an arithmetic error in the original draft: it asserted an 18px glyph at 1.5 m subtends what "6–7px
does at 40 cm", but 18 × (40/150) = **4.8px**. The 6–7px figure corresponds to a ~50–60 cm reading
distance, not 40 cm. Nobody flagged it; it surfaced only because the fix forced a recomputation.
That is the argument for stating assumptions numerically — "large enough to read at distance" cannot
be checked, and this one was wrong.
