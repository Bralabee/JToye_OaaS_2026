<objective>
Publish the retention schedule and close the second half of D-08's loop: the manifest-to-prose gate,
with **no new script**.

Purpose: 31-06 landed `docs/retention-manifest.json` and the tree-to-manifest gate. This plan renders
the page and adds the manifest-to-prose rows to the existing `scripts/gates/claims.manifest`, whose
engine is already wired in `docs-freshness.yml` — so this half costs a handful of TAB-separated rows
and no new `check-*.sh`, which is what keeps the gate-enforcement sequencing hazard entirely out of
this plan.

The engine's headline invariant is the falsifiability property D-08 needs: *"Every declared rule must
match at least once in its file. A rule that matches NOTHING is a FAILURE, not a pass. This is the
load-bearing rule: without it, deleting the sentence silently satisfies the gate."*

The table itself is the phase's one genuine no-analog UI problem. **Both existing table treatments in
this repo violate the contract**: `business-model-guide.tsx:227` uses `min-w-[640px]` inside
`overflow-x-auto`, guaranteeing a horizontal scrollbar at 375px; `dashboard/webhooks/page.tsx:303`
uses `hidden overflow-x-auto sm:block` plus a mobile card list, which duplicates the DOM — a trap
this repo has already filed twice as a product bug (#556, #593). A table that genuinely fits at 375px
does not exist here yet.

Output: one component, one route, the claims rows, and a browser assertion that the table fits.
</objective>

THE TABLE CONTRACT (UI-SPEC S2a) — four columns: **Data category · Retention period · Lawful basis ·
How it's enforced**:
  · one real `<table>` with a `<caption>`, `<th scope="col">` on headers, `<th scope="row">` on the
    category cell
  · the fourth column is not decoration — it is what makes 31-06's gate possible. Values are exactly
    `Automated` or `Operational`, rendered as a badge with a **text label**, never colour alone
  · **no placeholder ever ships.** A published `TBD` in a retention schedule is a defect, not a draft
  · **do NOT restyle to `display: block` at narrow widths** — that strips table semantics in several
    screen readers, turning a legally operative schedule into an unlabelled run of text
  · **do NOT duplicate the data** as a second mobile-only `<dl>` or card list
  · **the contract is that it FITS**: cells wrap (no `white-space: nowrap`), the period column is
    short ("6 years", "30 days"), and there is **no horizontal scrollbar at 375px** — assertable as
    `scrollWidth <= clientWidth`

    does overflow
  · shadcn's `Table` wraps in `<div className="relative w-full overflow-auto">` with **no** `role`,
    `aria-label` or `tabindex` (`components/ui/table.tsx:9`). Extend it; do not silently rely on it.
    Its default is `caption-bottom`; the marketing table overrides to `caption-top`. Pick one and say
    why.

TABLE TEXT is 14px/1.4 with 14px/600 headers, and the table region may widen to `max-w-4xl` — a
