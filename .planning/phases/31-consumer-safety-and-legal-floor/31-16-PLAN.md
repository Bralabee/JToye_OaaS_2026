<objective>
Ship the essential-cookies notice and the consent gate — client-only, with **no table and no
migration** — and prove the gate can actually block and permit, which a zero-category gate cannot do
on its own.

**OPEN QUESTION RESOLVED — CLIENT-ONLY, NO TABLE.** RESEARCH answered CONTEXT's question 1 with
HIGH confidence and a positive control, and this plan takes that as binding. There is no
`notification_consent` table: V54 creates `notification_suppression` and `marketing_opt_in`, both with
`tenant_id UUID NOT NULL` and `recipient TEXT NOT NULL` under FORCE RLS with a
`tenant_id = current_tenant_id()` policy. A cookie-consent decision is taken by an **anonymous visitor
on the platform origin before any tenant context or identity exists** — there is no value for either
NOT NULL column, and with no GUC set the row would be invisible to every subsequent read. Beyond
that, the object being gated is a browser-executed script, and a server row cannot stop a `<script>`
from running. The correct tier is the client.

**The falsifiability problem is the whole risk here, and D-05 says so itself.** There are zero
non-essential cookies and zero analytics or tag scripts anywhere in `frontend/app`,
`frontend/components` or `frontend/lib`. A gate over zero categories **cannot fail as shipped**, so
shipping it without a fixture-category proof buys nothing. Both arms — block, then permit — are
mandatory, and a test that merely shows the banner absent proves nothing.

Output: a consent module, a notice, a dormant banner, and the two-arm proof.
</objective>

## Instrument notes

- **A zero-category gate is unfalsifiable as shipped.** That is D-05's own warning, and it is why the
  fixture category is mandatory rather than nice to have. A test showing only that the banner is
  absent proves nothing at all.
- **The `storage` event fires only in OTHER tabs.** A same-tab dismissal needs the CustomEvent, and
  the assertion for it is easy to omit because manual testing in one tab looks fine either way.
- **`localStorage.setItem` throws in Safari private mode.** The store analog does not guard it; the
  cart analog does. Take the guard from the right file.
