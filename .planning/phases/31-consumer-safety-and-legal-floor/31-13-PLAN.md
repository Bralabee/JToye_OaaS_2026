<objective>
Publish the conformance statement LGL-02 requires — partial, dated, with named exceptions and
remediation dates — and make its staleness a build failure rather than a discovery.

Purpose: D-12 is explicit that the claim is **partial** conformance. WCAG supports a
partial-conformance claim, and overclaiming accessibility is itself Equality Act exposure. The 2026-07-08
audit recorded accessibility as "essentially absent"; a page saying "fully conformant" on top of that
history would be the worst available outcome.

The statement is also where this phase's honest gaps get written down instead of disappearing. Three
categories feed it, and each is a decision made elsewhere in this phase:

  - **Out of scope by decision (D-09):** the authenticated vendor dashboard. Named, not claimed.
  - **Outside our control:** account registration is hosted by the identity provider on a different
    origin (`components/storefront/customer-signin-card.tsx:81-87` redirects out to it), and the card
    payment form is a Stripe-hosted embed rendered inside an in-scope page.
  - **Known and unfixed in this phase:** every finding axe cannot see and that this phase did not
    close — from the QA council's live list, plus any `jsx-a11y` rule 31-02 downgraded for
    `app/dashboard/**`. A "zero violations" gate shipping alongside an unlisted failure makes the
    statement wrong; naming the failure with a date makes it right.

**A11Y-08 is resolved as FIX, not as exception.** RESEARCH left it to the planner: eight checkout
inputs carry no `autocomplete` token, which is a WCAG 2.1 level **AA** failure (SC 1.3.5) on a
declared surface that axe cannot detect. It is eight attributes and low risk, so 31-14 fixes it. It
therefore does NOT appear as an exception here — but if 31-14 reports it unfixed, this statement must
gain it as a named exception with a date. That conditional is the point: the statement's accuracy is
downstream of what actually shipped.

Output: one declared constant, one page, and a gate that expires.
</objective>
