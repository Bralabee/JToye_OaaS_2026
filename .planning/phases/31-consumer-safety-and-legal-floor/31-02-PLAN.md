## Instrument notes

- **`grep -c` exits 1 on a zero count** — the DESIRED state of an absence check — and
  `cmd | grep -q X` under `pipefail` INVERTS on match via SIGPIPE. Always
  `out=$(grep -cF X f || true); grep -qx 0 <<< "$out"`.
- **eslint's LAST line is the FIXABLE count, not the verdict.** Read the exit code, not the summary.
- Capture `rc` on the same statement as its command.
- **Commit before any break arm**; `git checkout` restores from the INDEX and discards post-staging
  edits. Verify each restore by `git hash-object` against `git rev-parse HEAD:<path>`, never by
  `git diff --stat`.
- `docs/metrics.json` is generated and is deliberately absent from `files_modified`. Regenerate with
  `scripts/docs-freshness.sh --write`.
