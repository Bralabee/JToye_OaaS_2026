<objective>
Build the shell every legal page uses, and configure the two contact values without which a published
privacy notice would render a blank where UK GDPR requires the controller's identity.

Purpose: `frontend/app/legal/` contains exactly **one file** today — a 64-line Companies House
trading disclosure. D-06 turns `/legal` into an index with `/legal/privacy`, `/legal/cookies`,
`/legal/retention` and `/legal/accessibility` beneath it. Four pages sharing one shell is one place
to get the heading hierarchy, the anchors and the "last updated" line right, rather than four.

The blocking content gap is real and measured: `getCompanyInfo().registeredOffice` defaults to `""`
and `NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_REGISTERED_OFFICE` is set **nowhere** — not in `.env.example`, not in
`docker-compose.full-stack.yml`, not in `frontend/Dockerfile`. UK GDPR Art. 13(1)(a)-(b) requires the
controller's identity **and contact details** in the privacy notice, and the UI-SPEC's F15 asserts
the contact route must resolve. A notice that renders a blank address, or a contact link that goes
nowhere, fails its own requirement. These are business facts, not engineering choices — hence the
checkpoint.

Output: two components, the contact configuration wired through all three build-arg sites, and
`/legal` converted to an index. The four content pages are 31-11, 31-12 and 31-13.
</objective>
