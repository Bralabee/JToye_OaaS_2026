<objective>
Put the order's allergen set in front of the customer before they commit, require an explicit
acknowledgement, and make the refusal audible — plus close the two checkout accessibility failures
axe cannot see.

**This is the one item in this phase that can injure someone.** Precision here is not polish.

Purpose: today there is **no consumer-allergen surface at checkout at all**. The guest cross-check
was removed on 2026-07-30 as special category data with no Article 9 condition, and the two remaining
allergen panels (`page.tsx:423-432` and `:523-535`) render only AFTER the order is created and are
guarded on `allergenWarnings.length > 0`, which `PublicStorefrontService.java:737-744` records as
**always empty**. They display nothing. D-02 adds a **pre-submit** block; it does not delete them.

Three sub-patterns have **zero precedent** in this codebase, each verified two-arm by the pattern
mapper: `aria-invalid` (0 hits in `app/` + `components/`, against a control of 12 `role="alert"`
hits), `aria-describedby` on a form field (0 — the two production hits are Radix dialog internals),
and programmatic focus-move to an invalid field (0 — the only `.focus()` in the app is the dish
modal's focus-return). All three are new work, and all three are what make a refusal accessible.

**A11Y-08 is fixed here, not excepted.** Eight checkout inputs carry no `autocomplete` token — a
WCAG 2.1 level AA failure (SC 1.3.5) on a declared surface that axe **cannot detect**. A
zero-violation gate shipping alongside an unlisted 1.3.5 failure would make 31-13's conformance
statement wrong. Eight attributes, low risk: fix it, and assert it with a source test rather than
relying on a scanner that is blind to it.

Output: one component, the checkout gate, and three tests.
</objective>
