Make customer emails identify the shop as the seller and carry the durable record of the order's
allergens (#789 email half, #785 email half; D-08, D-13).

deliver it", and carry no allergen record — so the durable-medium confirmation the CCR requires does
not exist, and the email cannot serve as the customer's record of what was acknowledged.

built from a listener-side context; unit and Testcontainers tests.
