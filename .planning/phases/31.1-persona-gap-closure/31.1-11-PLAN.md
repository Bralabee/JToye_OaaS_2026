<objective>
Delete the erased customer's sign-in account and tell them the erasure is done (#777, D-03, D-22).

Purpose: after #777's data fix (31.1-02) the person can still sign in with the realm account and see
their (now anonymised) orders, and nobody tells them anything. D-03 deletes the customer-realm user
after commit, best-effort and retried, the way #102/V49 deprovisions vendor users; the request is not
complete until that is confirmed. D-22 turns the Keycloak admin seam on in compose so this runs on the
canonical runtime.

Output: V72, two KeycloakAdminClient methods re-proved on Boot 4, CustomerAccountDeletionService, the
worker's completion gate and retry, DsarOutcomeMailer, compose/k8s config and tests. The live "can no
longer sign in" proof is in 31.1-30.
</objective>
