# Phase 31.1 Plan 10: Trader identity (the legal entity customers buy from) Summary

**V71 `trader_identity` with a V65-shape append-only `_aud`, both under FORCE RLS, plus a GROUP_ADMIN upsert API at `/api/v1/trader-identity` (field-level RFC 7807, UK postcode and GB VAT canonicalised) and a "Business details shown to customers" form on the vendor onboarding page.**

## Accomplishments

- A tenant has at most one legal entity: `UNIQUE(tenant_id)`, the company number read from `vendor_onboarding` and never stored twice, no contact columns (contact stays on `Shop.phone`/`Shop.email`), no Flyway backfill (D-10, D-11; the seeder and e2e backfill belong to 31.1-12).
- The database is the wall. Under a NOSUPERUSER role with tenant A's GUC pinned:
  - A sees its own row (precondition) and not B's;
  - an UPDATE of B's row matches 0 rows, while the same UPDATE matches A's row (control);
  - an INSERT stamped with another tenant is refused with 42501, while an own-tenant INSERT is accepted (control);
  - the audit trail is readable by A and not by B, and a forged foreign-tenant audit INSERT is refused with 42501.
- The API enforces GROUP_ADMIN on PUT (typed 403 `shop-access-denied`, nothing written) and lets every tenant user read. A 404 names the missing record. A `B` in `X-Tenant-Id` alongside A's JWT reads and writes A's row only.
- Validation holds at every boundary, as listed in the coverage block. Three shops resolve to one entity. Two identical PUTs leave one row, with version advanced by 1.
- The vendor form loads, validates and saves:
  - required fields are `aria-required`;
  - errors render in `role="alert"`, the input is `aria-invalid` with `aria-describedby`, and focus moves to the first error;
  - a save is confirmed in a persistent polite live region, and the identity is then re-read (the stored postcode and VAT number are shown in canonical form).
