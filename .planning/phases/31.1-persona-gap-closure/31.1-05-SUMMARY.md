# Phase 31.1 Plan 05: Storage-Key Registry and Sign-Out Teardown (#840) Summary

**One registry, `lib/client-storage-keys.ts`, now drives both the cookie policy's storage tables and the explicit sign-out teardown. Signing out removes the order list (order numbers plus the email each was placed with), every shop's saved checkout email, the last-signin stamp, the baskets and the tab's tracking email. A lapse removes none of them. A source scan fails the build on any `setItem` key the registry does not declare.**

## Accomplishments

- **The registry.** `CLIENT_STORAGE_KEYS` has 17 entries: 12 localStorage (two of them `<shop>`-suffixed prefixes) and 5 sessionStorage. Each entry carries its area, purpose, lifetime, `personal` and `clearedOnSignOut`. Key constants are imported from `cart-identity`, `consent` and `order-history`. Literals are used only where the owner is private, is a client module, or imports the registry; the scan proves each literal matches its writer.
- **The cookie policy.** Both storage tables map the registry. The page now:
  - discloses `jtoye-customer-last-signin`;
  - says the order list holds an email address and is not guest-only;
  - says "three items hold an email address" (it said two);
  - states what Sign out removes, in the row lifetimes, the prose and the "how to delete" bullet.
- **The teardown.** `clearSignedOutState()` (explicit sign-out only) calls `removeStorageEntries(PERSONAL_KEYS_CLEARED_ON_SIGN_OUT)`, which collects and then removes, per area. `clearMarker()` (the lapse path) is untouched, and its comments are kept.
- **The source scan.** It finds 20 `setItem` call sites and resolves them to 12 localStorage keys and 5 sessionStorage keys, all registered. Two sites cannot be resolved statically (`consent.ts` `writeRaw(key)` and `use-stored-state.ts`); both are named in `CALL_SITE_KEYS` with the keys they write. A stale map entry fails, and so does a registry entry that nothing writes (over-disclosure).
- **Retention.** R-12 and R-13 now read "Until you sign out or clear your browser's site data", in both the manifest and the retention page's transcribed rows.
- **Browser spec.** `e2e/storage-keys-signout.spec.ts`: register, place a cash order, require every key registered, run a non-vacuity control, sign out through the nav, then require every personal key gone by value.

## Issues Encountered

- The `block-base-python` guard refused `/usr/bin/python3 <script>` for the RED-record converter. That blocked command also took its jest run with it, so the first RED record was never written. The converter was redone in node (`red2rec.mjs`, scratchpad), which needs no Python, and jest was re-run.
- **Finding, not fixed (owner call):** nothing in the app reads `jtoye-guest-orders`. `getLocalOrders()` is called only by `saveLocalOrder()` (`rg -uu`, rc=0, one reader, itself). The list of order numbers and emails is personal data written on every order and shown nowhere. Its disclosed purpose ("so you can find them again") is not served by any current surface. There are two honest resolutions:
  - stop writing it (data minimisation);
  - build the reader, for example My Orders for guests.

  Either is a product decision outside this plan. It is now at least disclosed, and removed on sign-out.
