Wire the checkout to the server's acknowledgement contract and show the customer what was recorded
(#784, #785 client half; #812 checkout field; D-05, D-08, D-15).

checkout would hit that refusal on every order. This plan sends the shown set, recovers honestly from
a 409, shows the recorded set on the confirmation, and adds the dedicated allergy note field.

#785 race spec (run live in 31.1-30).
