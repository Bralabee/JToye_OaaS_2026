# Phase 31.1 Plan 12: Trader-identity go-live gate Summary

**A mandatory `TRADER_IDENTITY` onboarding gate means no shop is published unless its tenant's legal name and address are on file, a company shows its number, and the shop has an email address. The GO_LIVE guard requires the gate PASSED by type. Go-live materialises or refreshes the gate row and keeps it when the guard refuses. The vendor page says what to add and where. The demo and e2e shops are backfilled through tenant-pinned writes.**

## Accomplishments

- `TraderIdentityGate` is automatic and mandatory for every model. It reads the identity through `TraderIdentityService.findForTenant`, which refuses an unpinned tenant, and the shop through the tenant-scoped finder.
  - It fails with a reason that names the remedy, in this order: no shop, no identity ("Add your business details…"), an incomplete legal name or address, a COMPANY with no company number, and a shop with no email ("Add an email address to your shop…").
  - When it passes, its evidence records which details are present (`legal_name`, `geographic_address`, `entity_type`, `company_number` present or not_required, `vat_number`, `shop_email`, `shop_phone`) and never their values.
- `goLiveGuard` = APPROVE precondition AND PASSED `ALLERGEN_DATA_COMPLETE` AND PASSED `TRADER_IDENTITY`, each matched by type. A WAIVED row satisfies APPROVE but never GO_LIVE, and an absent row never satisfies either.
- `refreshTraderIdentityGate` runs on GO_LIVE and REINSTATE. It creates the row when it is absent (`mandatory` comes from the gate bean) and re-evaluates it whatever its stored status. `goLive()` is `noRollbackFor` the guard veto, so a refused go-live keeps the refreshed rows.
- The vendor page and the approvals queue label the gate "Business details". A FAILED row explains why the details are needed and links to the business-details form (offered in every state, because the form is always mounted) and to the shop editor for the email. A go-live 400 re-reads the onboarding, so the row it just recorded appears on screen.
- The dev seeder gives the demo tenant a "Mama Ade's Kitchen (demo)" sole-trader identity at 48 Rye Lane / Peckham / London / SE15 5BS, and gives each of the 3 published demo shops the email `kitchen@<slug>.example.com`.
  - It only creates; nothing already present is overwritten.
  - It runs in the seed transaction with the demo tenant pinned.
- The e2e script writes the same values for the vendor tenant in one transaction after `set_config`, and refuses to continue (VOID) when the RLS-backfill trap fires.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] A refused go-live would have rolled back the gate row that explains the refusal**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (the plan's behaviour "gate row FAILED with a reason naming business details").
- **Issue:** the refresh runs inside the go-live transaction, and the guard veto rolled it back. An in-flight onboarding (no row) therefore stayed with no row after every refusal: the vendor saw a toast and no gate, and Task 2's remediation could never render.
- **Fix:** `@Transactional(noRollbackFor = InvalidStateTransitionException.class)` on `goLive()`. The veto is thrown before any other write. Break arm D shows the effect.
- **Commit:** 44475f10.

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] The page did not re-read the onboarding after a go-live 400**
- **Found during:** Task 2.
- **Issue:** the page kept the pre-go-live gate list, so the newly recorded TRADER_IDENTITY row stayed invisible until a reload.
- **Fix:** `loadOnboarding(false)` on a 400, tested by the third new page test and proven by break arm N.
- **Commit:** 3b1241cb.

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `approvals/page.tsx` has its own `Record<GateType, …>`**
- **Issue:** this file is not in the plan's file list, and the build fails without the member.
- **Fix:** added "Business details", with a code note that a WAIVE there satisfies approval but never go-live.
- **Commit:** 3b1241cb.

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] The full suite after Task 1's GREEN found 4 red tests outside the plan's verify scope**
- **Tests:**
