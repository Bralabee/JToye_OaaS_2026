<objective>
Give every tenant a place to declare the legal entity customers buy from (#789 P0, D-10, D-11, D-20 groundwork).

Purpose: the platform has no legal-entity field at all; shops publish a trading name and premises
only. CCR 2013 Sch 2 and the E-Commerce Regulations require the seller's identity, a geographic
address and contact details before purchase. This plan builds the tenant-level record, its secured
API and the vendor form. The onboarding gate that requires it (D-12) is 31.1-12; the customer-facing
seller block is 31.1-24; emails are 31.1-25.

Output: V71 (table, _aud, RLS, gate CHECK rewrite), entity/repository/service/controller/DTOs,
a NOSUPERUSER RLS test, the vendor form and the regenerated OpenAPI snapshot.
</objective>
