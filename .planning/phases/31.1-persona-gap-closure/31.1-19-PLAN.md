<objective>
Give the vendor the D-09 warning where they edit the product, record cross-contact separately, and
ask for the production date when printing a label (#787 vendor half; #861 vendor half; D-09, D-16, D-17).

Purpose: the backend now returns a typed warning (31.1-06), a may-contain mask and a production-dated
label (31.1-14). Without this plan the vendor never sees the warning, cannot enter may-contain, and the
label still defaults silently; and the vendor's own list still says "No allergens" for the persona's dish.

Output: the shared allergen copy module, the vendor form and list changes, the label dialog and jest
proofs. Browser click-through in 31.1-30.
</objective>
