# Phase 31.1 Plan 19: Vendor allergen warning, may-contain and production-dated labels Summary

**The vendor now sees #787 where it happens. A save whose ingredients name MILK with Milk unticked shows "The ingredients mention Milk, but Milk is not ticked" with "Tick Milk" and "Keep as it is", and the products list reads "Ingredients name: MILK – not ticked" instead of "No allergens". May-contain is recorded in its own fieldset as mayContainMask. Every label download asks for the production date, defaulting to the UK date, and shows a 422 under the field.**

## Accomplishments

- **#787, vendor side.** The save still succeeds, as 31.1-06 designed it. The dialog now stays open on the saved product with an amber `role=alert` above the form, and focus moves to it. Each line names an allergen ("The ingredients mention Milk, but Milk is not ticked") and has a "Tick Milk" button, which sets the bit and saves the same product with PUT. The alert repeats the storefront wording the customer sees until the two agree, then offers "Keep as it is", which closes and records nothing.
- **Pitfall 11 closed.** The vendor list no longer prints the bare "No allergens". A product with a warning shows `Ingredients name: MILK – not ticked` beside any declared badges. An empty declaration with no warning reads `No allergens declared`.
- **Pitfall 6.** The ingredients field explains both emphasis forms the server reads (CAPITALS, `**double asterisks**`), with the persona's own line as the example.
- **D-16.** A "May contain (cross-contact)" fieldset of the 14 allergens has its own helper text and a dashed, neutral style. It is held in its own state and sent as `mayContainMask`. Neither mask is computed from the other. An untouched fieldset stays `null` (not recorded), and `0` stays `0`.
- **D-17.** The label button keeps its accessible name but now opens "Print allergen label". The date input defaults to today in Europe/London and is capped there; at 2026-10-03T23:30Z the default is 2026-10-04. The download requests `/api/v1/products/{id}/label?productionDate=YYYY-MM-DD`. A future date is refused before any request. A 422 `errors/invalid-production-date` is read out of the blob error body and shown under the field with `aria-invalid`, and nothing is downloaded.
- **One copy source.** `lib/allergen-copy.ts` holds every phrase, ready for 31.1-21's storefront (`undeclaredIngredientCopy`, `mayContainCopy`).

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter. The two that most affect later plans:

- **31.1-21 imports from `lib/allergen-copy.ts`.** The storefront line is `undeclaredIngredientCopy(names)` and the may-contain line is `mayContainCopy(names)`. Each returns `null` when there is nothing to print. Do not restate them.
- **An absent `allergenWarnings` is treated as "no line".** The list then shows the declared badges, or "No allergens declared" for an empty mask. That is a true statement about the declaration. 31.1-06 notes the field can be absent only on a pre-deploy by-id cache entry, which the list endpoint never serves.
