# Phase 31.1 Plan 25: Order emails name the seller and carry the allergen record (#789, #785) Summary

**Customer order emails now come From "{shop} via J'Toye" with Reply-To the shop. Every email names the seller's legal identity, address and contact details and says J'Toye is only the platform. The order-received email is now the durable record: it carries the allergen record (D-08) and the CCR reg 28(1)(c) no-cancellation statement. All of it is built in the AMQP listener while the tenant is pinned, and is proven under real FORCE RLS.**

## Accomplishments

- **One context, built under the tenant (Pitfall 8).** `OrderStateChangeListener` builds a `CustomerEmailContext` once per email, inside the existing `TenantContext` block. It reads:
  - the shop, by `findByIdAndTenantId`;
  - the seller, by `TraderIdentityService.findPublicSeller`;
  - the V63 recorded set and flags, by `OrderAllergenSnapshot.viewOf`;
  - the V69 acknowledgement, through `AllergenCatalog.namesFor`;
  - `placedVia` and the fulfilment type.

  The `@Async` send looks nothing up. `OrderStateChangeEvent` is byte-identical to the plan base.
- **The From header and Reply-To.** Every email is a UTF-8 plain-text `MimeMessage`.
  - From is `InternetAddress(notification.email.from, sanitised(shop) + " via J'Toye", "UTF-8")`. With no shop name it is "J'Toye" alone.
  - Reply-To is the shop email when it parses strictly as exactly one address. Otherwise there is no Reply-To header.
- **The body.** "We" wording that described the seller now names the shop. Every status carries the 31.1-24 seller block, in the same field order with the same labels, and the platform statement. Order received also carries:
  - the allergen record, as three separate statements (null is never written as "none");
  - the cancellation statement.

  The footer reads "This email was sent by J'Toye on behalf of {shop}." and gives J'Toye Digital Ltd, England & Wales, company number 16471464. It adds "Registered office: …" only when `COMPANY_REGISTERED_OFFICE` is set.
- **One source of words.** The Java constants are compared with `seller-block.tsx`, `recorded-allergen-set.tsx` and `order-allergen-panel.tsx` by a parity test. Break arm 3 shows that a paraphrase fails it.
- **Logging (ASVS V7).** The service logs the order number and template name only. The listener logs the status and order number only. A mail failure logs the exception class only.

## Issues Encountered

- **Assumption A2, measured.** The plan expected the sanitiser to be what stops injection. In fact Jakarta Mail's header folding already neutralises a CR/LF in an `InternetAddress` personal name. The sanitiser is kept as defence in depth: it is what keeps the BODY's seller lines unforgeable, and it strips NUL, TAB, NEL and U+2028 from the display name.
- **Observation, not filed under the admission test.** The advisory flag line mirrors the storefront's `allergenFlagCopy` exactly, including "Ask the kitchen before you order." That sentence is odd in a post-order email, and it already appears on the post-order confirmation page. Changing it belongs to whoever owns that copy (31.1-29 or QA), in both places at once, so that the parity test keeps them identical.
