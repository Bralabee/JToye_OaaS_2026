## Decisions Made

- **Uniqueness key.**
  - What "every in-scope route appears exactly once" means here: each surface appears once. Labels are unique, and (path, label) pairs are unique.
  - Why not paths: the existing data already listed `/shop/[slug]` twice, for the shop page and the dish panel, and the cash confirmation is a state of `/shop/[slug]/checkout`. Making paths unique would mean dropping surfaces or inventing URLs.
- **Fix rather than publish.** Every violation axe found at page load was local and fixed. The contrast exception still covers states a page-load scan never reaches, such as error text and empty states.
- **The skip link became a gate assertion.** The removed exception's comment says the gate now checks it on every surface. Arm B proves the check can fail.
- **The gate imports the statement.** Test titles, failure messages and the coupling test read the standard and scope from `lib/accessibility-statement.ts`, so the two cannot describe different things.
