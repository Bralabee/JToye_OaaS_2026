<objective>
Make the cookie policy list every key the site stores, and make sign-out remove the personal ones (#840).

Purpose: `jtoye-guest-orders` (order number + email) is written for signed-in orders and survives
sign-out on a shared device, and `jtoye-customer-last-signin` (the customer's id) is not disclosed.
A hand-maintained policy list already drifted once (31-11 measured 5 sessionStorage keys, not 2).
One registry consumed by both the policy page and the sign-out code makes the disclosure structural.

Output: the registry, the policy rendering from it, sign-out clearing proven through the transition,
a source scan that fails on an unregistered key, the R-13 retention wording, and the Playwright spec
(run live in 31.1-30).
</objective>
