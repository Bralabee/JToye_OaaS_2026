<objective>
Make a verified Article 17 request actually erase a storefront customer (#777, P0, D-02).

Purpose: storefront orders never create a `customers` row, and `GdprService.eraseSubjectByDigest`
iterates `customers` only, so on the dev runtime every storefront subject's erasure matched nothing
and was marked completed (`tenantsErased=0`). This plan extends the digest match to the addresses on
`orders` and `reviews` in each pinned tenant and anonymises through ONE shared routine, writing a
guest erasure record that V68 allows.

Output: V68, a refactored `GdprService` (one anonymisation core, two entry points), tenant-predicated
repository methods, and `DsarGuestErasureRlsIntegrationTest` recorded red then green.
Account deletion (D-03), the completion email and the encrypted address are 31.1-07/-11, not here.
</objective>
