<objective>
Turn the allergy request from a buried free-text note into a field the shop must acknowledge (#812, D-15 backend).

Purpose: "My child is allergic to peanuts and sesame — please confirm" went to COMPLETED as plain
NOTES, and nothing recorded that anyone read it. D-15 makes it a dedicated field, prominent to the
kitchen, acknowledged by a named person at a recorded time, with the customer able to see it was read.

Output: V73, the request/entity/DTO fields, the acknowledgement endpoint, the tracking flag, the
erasure scrub, the Article 9 record, the OpenAPI snapshot and the acceptance IT. The checkout field
(31.1-15), tracking display (31.1-18) and KDS / vendor view (31.1-22) are separate plans.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions

- **Edge PGC-812 (unclassified by the probe, carried as an assumption):** the requirement does not say whether the note can be edited after the order is placed, whether an acknowledgement can be withdrawn, or whether the vendor must reply in words. This plan assumes: not editable after placement (it is part of the order as submitted), the acknowledgement is first-write-wins and not reversible, and the acknowledgement records "read" only — a written reply is not built. Each is recorded in the SUMMARY for owner review.
- **Research Open Question 6 (not ruled by the owner):** the free-text note is acceptable under the vendor-as-controller position with an inline notice; recorded in `docs/legal/article-9-allergen-basis.md` and marked for adviser review.
- **Assumption A1:** `@JsonInclude(NON_NULL)` keeps the fingerprint unchanged on Jackson 3 (measured by Phase 38's golden test in Task 3).
- Canon referral (not minted): stored-XSS safety of the note text is the existing plain-text rendering (goods P2-KYL-P1) and `/gsd-secure-phase`.
