<objective>
Record cross-contact risk separately, date labels from production rather than download, and render
dates the UK way (#861, D-16, D-17).

Purpose: there is no "may contain" field at all; the label's use-by is computed from the moment the
PDF is downloaded ("Use by: 5 Oct 2026" two days after an order dated "Oct 3, 2026, 11:10:25 PM"),
which is weak evidence of how long food has been out. D-16 adds a separate mask, D-17 asks for the
production date and formats every label date en-GB.

Output: V74, the mask through entity/DTO/request/public DTO, the label service and endpoint changes,
the regenerated label golden, the IT and the OpenAPI snapshot. The vendor form (31.1-19) and the
storefront line (31.1-21) are separate; order-record date formatting on vendor views is 31.1-22.
</objective>
