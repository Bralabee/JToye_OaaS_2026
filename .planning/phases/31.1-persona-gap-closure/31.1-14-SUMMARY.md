# Phase 31.1 Plan 14: May-contain mask and production-dated en-GB labels (#861) Summary

**Products now record cross-contact risk as their own mask (V74), which is never merged into the declared allergens. The PPDS label prints a separate "May contain" line for the risks that are not already declared, prints the production date, and counts use-by from that date rather than from the download. Every label date reads the UK way ("5 October 2026"). The storefront contract carries the same may-contain list.**

## Accomplishments

- **#861 reproduced and closed at the API and on the PDF.** On the unfixed tree the label ignored `productionDate` and printed `Use by: 8 Oct 2026`, the download day plus shelf life. Now the label for `?productionDate=2026-10-03` (Milk declared, may-contain Sesame and Milk, shelf life 2) reads `Produced: 3 October 2026`, `Use by: 5 October 2026` and `May contain: Sesame`. The text is read out of the PDF with `PdfTextExtractor`.
- **V74.** It adds `products.may_contain_mask INT`, `CHECK (NULL OR 0..16383)`, and the `products_aud` mirror in the same migration. It has a V63-style header covering the never-merged rule, NULL as "not recorded" versus 0 as "none", and why there is no backfill or tenant loop.
- **Kept apart by construction.** `MayContainAllergens.undeclaredNames` is the one rule the label and `PublicProductDto.mayContainAllergens` both use. Nothing writes the may-contain mask into `allergen_mask`, the order snapshot or the acknowledgement. The IT proves it by SQL, and the merge arm shows that check can fail.
- **"Today" is the UK date.** A new `ClockConfig` bean (`Clock.system(Europe/London)`) is injected into `ProductLabelService`, which reads `LocalDate.now(clock.withZone(UK_ZONE))` once per request. At 23:30Z on 3 October (00:30 BST) the default production date is 4 October, and the UTC-date arm turns that case red.
- **Typed 422 for an untrue date.** `InvalidProductionDateException` maps to `errors/invalid-production-date` with `field: "productionDate"`. It covers a date after today in London, and a durability date (use-by or best-before) before today. A use-by of today is allowed.
- **The label still fits its page.** The page-count assertion caught that adding two lines pushed the business address onto page 2. The `Ingredients:` heading now runs inline, and production plus durability share one 12pt line. One line of ingredients plus may-contain fits one page.
