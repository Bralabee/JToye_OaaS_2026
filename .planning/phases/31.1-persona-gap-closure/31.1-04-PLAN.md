<objective>
Stop loading Stripe's script and fraud cookies on a checkout that takes no payment (#793, D-Discretion).

Purpose: importing `loadStripe` from the package's default entry injects js.stripe.com as a module
side effect, so every cash checkout sets `__stripe_mid` (1 year) and calls m.stripe.network while
the page says no payment is taken online — a PECR breach and a contradiction of the cookie policy.
The `/pure` entry injects nothing until called.

Output: the checkout loads Stripe lazily and only for a real card payment, the five checkout test
files mock the `/pure` entry, a jest proof of both paths, and the Playwright acceptance spec (run live
in 31.1-30).
</objective>
