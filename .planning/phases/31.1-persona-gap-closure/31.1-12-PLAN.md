<objective>
Make the statutory seller details a precondition of going live (#789 P0, D-12, D-20).

Purpose: the onboarding state machine is the only thing that publishes a shop, so the only reliable
way to guarantee "no shop goes live without the D-10 details" is a gate the go-live guard requires.
The research found two silent failure modes this plan closes: the V43 CHECK (rewritten in V71 by
31.1-10) and in-flight onboardings that have no row for a new gate and would pass vacuously.

Output: the gate, the guard and refresh changes, the frontend gate surface, the seeded demo details,
and an integration test with the vacuity and WAIVED arms.
</objective>
