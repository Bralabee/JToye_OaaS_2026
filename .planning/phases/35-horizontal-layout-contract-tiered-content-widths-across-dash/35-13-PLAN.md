<objective>
Put the change in front of the person who asked for it.

This phase started from owner feedback, quoted in CONTEXT.md: *"not enough of the real estate is being
utilised… it feels narrow and confined to the middle… being able to utilise at least two thirds of any
screen's width for the web version is more mature and professional."* Every measurement since has been
about whether the numbers are defensible. None of them answers whether the product now feels right,
and that is the acceptance criterion the phase was opened against.

There is also a specific defect class that only a person catches. RESEARCH names it: a centred narrow
layout that read as deliberate becomes a stretched band with a lonely heading — green tests, worse
product. The warning sign is content hugging the left edge of a now-wider band. No assertion in this
phase looks for that.

Purpose: a phase whose origin was a subjective judgement does not close on a green suite.

Output: a recorded decision, and any findings raised at the gate.
</objective>

  <what-built>
    A declared four-tier horizontal width contract, replacing the inherited shadcn 1400px default that
    was applied uniformly to four different kinds of content.

    Shell, about 1700px, on the dashboard chrome — matching the 1680 to 1720 band where Stripe's
    dashboard and Square's shells independently sit.
    Index, fluid to the shell cap, on the dashboard resource lists — matching the documented
    full-width-for-many-columns pattern from Polaris, Carbon, GitLab and Lightspeed.
    Detail, about 1100px, on order detail, forms and the onboarding flow — matching the 1016 to 1136
    reading-column cluster, so reading surfaces did not get dragged wider by the phase. This is the one
    tier that makes surfaces NARROWER than they are today: about 236px at 1920 and 2560, about 20px at
    1440. Deliberate, and called out at step 6 below so it is confirmed rather than discovered.
    Marketing, 1280px, on the landing and marketing routes — which also fixes the landing page's
    content being inset 128px from its own header rail.
    Prose stays at its existing measure, untouched.

    Also fixed in passing: the shop detail skeleton was 1280px while the content replacing it was
    896px, so the page narrowed by 384px on hydration.

    Everything below the large breakpoint is byte-identical, proven by diffing the generated
    stylesheet rather than by looking at a phone.
  </what-built>
