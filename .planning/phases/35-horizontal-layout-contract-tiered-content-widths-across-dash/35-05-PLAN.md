<objective>
Cap the dashboard's reading and form surfaces at the Detail width so the phase widens what benefits
from width and leaves alone what does not.

CONTEXT.md section 3, third finding: reading and detail columns cluster tightly at 1016 to 1136px
across Square and Linear, and prose-measure guidance (45 to 75 characters) is why. Widening a
settings form or an order detail to 1700px would be a regression, not an improvement. This plan is
the half of the phase that protects against its own enthusiasm.

Purpose: after plan 35-02 these three pages inherit a 1700px band. Without this plan they would be
300px wider than they were before the phase started, which is the opposite of the contract.

Output: three dashboard surfaces declaring the Detail tier, plus a recorded exceptions ledger for the
surfaces that are deliberately narrower than it.
</objective>

APPLICATION SHAPE — in place, on the page's existing root element. Add `data-width-tier="detail"`
AND the detail class from WIDTH_TIER_CLASS, plus `mx-auto` so the narrower band centres inside the
wider Shell band rather than hugging the left edge. Do not add `w-full`: the element is block-level
inside a block parent, so its width already resolves to the available width clamped by the cap, and
every marketing surface in this tree already centres correctly with a bare auto-margin and cap.

Do not add a wrapper node. All three pages already return a single root element.
</interfaces>

<tier_is_a_ceiling>
A CONTRACT RULE this plan establishes, and plan 35-11 writes into the contract document:

**A tier is a ceiling, not a target.** A surface that is already narrower than its tier for a stated
reason keeps its measure and is recorded as an exception. Widening a 672px Stripe Connect status
panel of three paragraphs to 1100px because "it is a Detail surface" would be a regression, and the
Incremental Betterment doctrine names that class of change directly: it trades away a working good
to satisfy a rule.

The exceptions in the dashboard, all verified present in the tree and all to be LEFT ALONE:
  - the Stripe Connect return and refresh outcome panel, shared by both routes, at 672px
  - every Radix dialog width (portal-rendered, outside the page container, so the page tier cannot
    reach them and their width is a modal-ergonomics decision, not a layout-contract one)
  - the truncation clamps on table cells and empty-state copy measures
</tier_is_a_ceiling>
