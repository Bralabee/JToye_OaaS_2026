<objective>
Turn the three marketing surfaces that already render at 1280px from coincidentally correct into
declared, and fix the storefront skeleton that narrows its page by 384px on hydration.

Two distinct jobs, one plan, because both are zero-width-delta edits on public surfaces.

**Job one — declaration.** PATTERNS.md F-2: the Marketing tier is already about 75% shipped. The
operator route, the business-model guide, the competitive teardown and both storefront rails already
render at exactly 1280px, but they do so because a stock scale token happens to equal that number,
not because anything declares it. UIX-07 asks for a DECLARED contract; a coincidence that nothing
asserts is the same class of problem as the inherited 1400 this phase exists to remove.

**Job two — the hydration jump.** PATTERNS.md A-12, an existing defect the inventory surfaced:
`app/shop/[slug]/loading.tsx:14` renders its skeleton band at 1280px while the content that replaces
it (`shop-detail-client.tsx`) renders at 896px. **The page narrows by 384px the moment real content
arrives.** This is user decision D-05. It is a one-line change and it is exactly the class of defect a
width contract exists to prevent.

Purpose: no surface in this plan changes width. That is what makes it safe to run in parallel with
plan 35-06, which owns the one surface that does.

Output: five surfaces declaring the Marketing tier, one skeleton aligned to its content, and A-11
recorded as deferred with its reason.
</objective>

<a11_deferral>
**A-11, `/shop/[slug]` menu width, is DEFERRED and this plan does not widen it.** Recorded here so it
is a decision rather than an omission.

The shop detail client renders its menu at 896px across nine sites in one file. PATTERNS argues it
could defensibly take the Detail tier (1100), citing Linear at 1136 and Lightspeed at 1100, and also
argues the opposite: 896 is within prose-measure territory, a menu row is a scannable line rather than
a grid, the surface is absent from CONTEXT's measured baseline table, and it is the highest-churn edit
available in the phase. The user's decision list scopes the public storefront to "unchanged", and
plan 35-05's contract rule settles the rest: **a tier is a ceiling, not a target**, so a surface
already narrower than its tier for a stated reason keeps its measure.

So `/shop/[slug]` keeps 896, the skeleton is aligned DOWN to match it, and neither gets a tier
attribute — an attribute would claim a tier the surface has not been assigned. Both files get a
comment saying the two must move together and that A-11 is deferred, and plan 35-10's gate asserts
the parity mechanically, because a comment is not an assertion.
</a11_deferral>
