<objective>
Tracer slice, layer 3 of the DPLY-01 path: the in-cluster platform the app needs before it can be reached — the
ingress controller (D-29), TLS issuance (D-07), the RabbitMQ operator (D-09), and the scoped CI permissions D-32
requires — authored and verified offline, applied later by plan 29-11.

Third-party manifests stay outside the kustomize targets (RESEARCH Pattern 3): gates discover targets at depth 2 and
render-golden compares staging/production byte-for-byte, so 1 MB of CRDs inside a kustomization would turn every
upstream bump into an unreviewable golden diff. Our own platform objects live under k8s/platform/ (depth 3: not a
gate target), applied by the bootstrap script with an explicit --context.

D-29 ruled Traefik v3 with its Ingress-NGINX provider and the existing nginx annotations as the source of truth. Two
facts measured at plan time (2026-10-18, doc.traefik.io v3.7 reference pages) differ from the research and are
recorded here so the executor re-measures rather than inherits: (1) the provider's support table now LISTS all four
annotations research found missing (proxy-buffer-size, proxy-buffers-number under Buffering; proxy-connect-timeout,
proxy-send-timeout under Timeout); (2) the provider has an `allowSnippetAnnotations` option (shown false in the
reference examples, default unstated) that gates configuration-snippet — with it off, the security headers in
k8s/base/ingress.yaml would be dropped silently (Pitfall 3, through a different door). The pinned version's page is
authoritative; the live fidelity probe in plan 29-15 decides.
