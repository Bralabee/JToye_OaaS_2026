<objective>
Deliver the half of DPLY-03 that involves a human: an Alertmanager that reaches a real inbox, and a
Grafana somebody can open on a phone at 2 a.m.

Two design facts drive this plan.

**Alertmanager has no native env-var substitution.** The compose analog says so in its own comment
and renders its config from a template. The same applies in Kubernetes: the Gmail app password
cannot be a literal in a ConfigMap, and `check-no-plaintext-secrets.sh` forbids a `kind: Secret` in
any kustomize build. So the config is a template rendered at pod start.

**L-3 needs the destination to be inspectable, without bypassing the real route.**
`check-alert-liveness.sh` VOIDs if it cannot inspect the alert destination, and D-17 makes that
destination Gmail, which has no HTTP API. Plan 29-03 settled the mechanism by measuring assumption
A1; this plan implements whatever that measurement chose. The single receiver carries both sinks —
same route, same grouping, same template — so L-3 still proves what it claims. The script's explicit
rejection of "a probe-only route with group_wait 0s" is respected because the route does not change.

D-19 also draws the exposure line: **Grafana alone** gets a public hostname (added in plan 29-08).
Prometheus and Alertmanager have no real authn of their own and stay cluster-internal. That is
worth an invariant, not a convention.

Purpose: "alerts a human" needs a human-reachable path and a dashboard.
Output: Alertmanager and Grafana in the render, real observability NetworkPolicies, and a CI
invariant that keeps the exposure line where D-19 drew it.

Branch: `phase-29-research`. Never commit to main.
</objective>
