Noun now primary: the network-policy ENGINE is Cilium (AKS-native, `--network-dataplane cilium --network-policy cilium`).
Until 2026-10-18 the requirement said only "an enforcing engine (#297)", a derived property; this plan turns it
into a chosen one at cluster creation.

standard NetworkPolicy only (RESEARCH §Alternatives); the manifests stay standard networking.k8s.io/v1 NetworkPolicy,
so promoting the engine adds no CRD dependency.

NetworkPolicy (no CiliumNetworkPolicy, no Calico GlobalNetworkPolicy), and this script's aks argv must carry
`--network-policy cilium`; the two together mean the engine and the policy dialect cannot drift apart.
