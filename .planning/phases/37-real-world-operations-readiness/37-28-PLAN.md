enforced lifetime (D-18, D-19, D-27; RWO-041, RWO-006).

Purpose: owner ruling D-27 chose a keyed digest on the order so "open orders per IP" is a query; because a pseudonymous identifier then
lives with the order, it falls under retention and Article 17 erasure.

</objective>
