<objective>
Bound how many open cash orders one customer identity can hold at a shop, and record whether the contact was verified (D-18;
RWO-041 UXT-041). The IP axis with its keyed digest (D-27) is 37-28; bulk reject is 37-29.

Purpose: anyone can place many fake cash orders in seconds with throwaway details. Caps per email and phone, a typed 429 that is
never auto-retried, and a stored "unverified contact" fact for the kitchen badge.

Output: V81 (all three D columns, reserved here so 37-28/37-29 add no migration), CashOrderCaps, the typed 429, contact_verified.
</objective>
