<feature>
  <name>Honest webhook auto-pause</name>
  <files>WebhookProperties, WebhookDeliveryWorker, WebhookDelivery, WebhookSubscriptionService, WebhookSubscriptionDto, EmailNotificationService, webhooks page</files>
  <behavior>
    - two events, each failing 4 attempts then succeeding -> subscription stays ACTIVE (today: AUTO_PAUSED after ~46 s)
    - three consecutive deliveries exhausting their attempts -> AUTO_PAUSED; one email to the tenant contact; a fourth queued event becomes HELD (today: FAILED)
    - resume -> HELD deliveries back to PENDING and delivered; no second email for the same pause
    - a success between failures resets the consecutive count
  </behavior>
  <implementation>Count on delivery exhaustion; hold instead of fail when not ACTIVE; email on the status transition only.</implementation>
</feature>
