person; machine-specific rules (role ceiling, human-only management, last-used) live in IntegrationCredentialService and
HumanCallerGuard.

integration_credential. What would force a promote: a rule that must differ for machines inside ShopAccessService itself (machine-only
rate limits, audit fields, or a role only machines may hold) — then shop_staff gains an explicit principal_kind.
Suggested invariant test: no shop_staff row whose user_id is a credential's service_account_user_id ever has role GROUP_ADMIN.
</assumption_delta_decision>
