<objective>
Let a vendor pause orders in one tap and enforce structured hours on the server, with one decision every surface reads
(D-15, D-25; RWO-022 UXT-022).

Purpose: there is no pause, and free-text hours fail open on the server while the client shows "Closed". The policy replaces
validateShopIsOpen, the storefront DTO carries the server's answer, and the pause is a typed, idempotent endpoint.

Output: ShopAvailabilityPolicy, ShopClosedException, pause endpoints, schedule write path, DTO fields, unit and IT tests, OpenAPI.
</objective>

  <behavior>
    - Policy (unit, fixed clocks): Mon 12:00 with mon 09:00-17:00 -> open; Mon 18:00 -> CLOSED_NOW, nextOpenAt Tue 09:00 (if Tue has a window); Fri 23:30 and Sat 01:30 with fri 18:00-02:00 -> open; all days [] -> CLOSED/NO_HOURS; LEGACY_ALWAYS_OPEN with no schedule -> open; pausedUntil in 10 min -> PAUSED with reopensAt; pausedIndefinitely -> PAUSED no reopensAt; the last Sunday of March 01:30 (clock change) and of October handled without an exception and with UK local times
    - IT: STAFF pauses 20 -> public shop openNow false, pausedUntil set; guest order -> 409 shop-closed reason PAUSED, no order and no idempotency reservation left; DELETE pause -> guest order 201
    - IT: ungranted user pause -> 403; minutes 15 -> 400; vendor POST /api/v1/orders during the pause -> 201
    - IT: an unparseable-legacy (NEEDS_REVIEW) day is closed: guest order on that day -> 409 CLOSED_TODAY (was 201 under fail-open)
  </behavior>
  <implementation>Policy reads Shop fields only; storefront and checkout call it; the schedule is written only through ShopService.</implementation>
</feature>
