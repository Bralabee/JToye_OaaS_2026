### The WR-01 / WR-02 question, answered plainly

**Is "the last-page control stays inside its card at 390 px" achieved and proven for realistic data? Yes.** I tested it directly rather than taking either report's word:

- I rewrote the page labels on the live orders page in Chromium and set the Pagination container width, using the real stylesheet and Work Sans 14 px. At a 308 px root (the 390 px case, 19.25rem), with the compact status reading "Page 19999 of 20000", the last button ends 20 px inside the root at 19rem and 44 px inside at 20.5rem. The status text wraps (`min-w-0`) rather than pushing a control out, so the 390 px result does not depend on how many pages there are. At 375 px (293 px root) only previous and next show.
- WR-01 is real but narrower than "overflows the card at four-digit page numbers". Measured at the exact thresholds: labels 1-3 and 98-100 fit with the 4 px margin (-4); labels 998/999/1000 overflow by **4 px at 21rem and 12 px at 27rem**; 8888-range labels by 24 and 44 px; 19998-20000 by 44 and 78 px. A control only leaves the card when the container sits within roughly 4 to 28 px above a threshold: container widths about 336 to 364 px (phones around 420 to 450 px wide, 3-page tier) and about 432 to 460 px (5-page tier). At 768 (398 px root) and 1024 and above there is more than 60 px of slack. Four-digit labels need the user to be paging at page 1,000 or beyond, which at 20 per page means a tenant with about 20,000 orders on one unfiltered list.
- I also note a smaller truth the review does not state: with five three-digit labels (for example 997 to 999 at 45 px) the 5-page tier at exactly 432 px is over by about 1 px, so even three-digit labels leave roughly no margin. Hence the coincidental-reliance flag.

So: the G-37-6 truth as the UAT reported it holds for the data a vendor has, and this is the same defect-3 shape the owner would hit only at a scale this product has not reached. I record it as an advisory warning rather than a gap, because it needs both a deep-paged list of at least ~20,000 rows and a container width inside a ~28 px band, and the stated truth (390 px) is unaffected. It is not "proven" beyond that: nothing in jest or the live spec steers the Pagination container to its own 19/21/27rem edges with wide labels, although the same spec does exactly that for the finance grids. The cheap hardening is in `coincidental_reliance_items`.

**WR-02 does not mean the containment check is vacuous.** The arm asserts only `failures.length > 0`, but the committed GREEN output of that arm (evidence section 4.1) lists `"Go to page 5" leaves the card: 361..393 vs 16..374` and `leaves the viewport: 361..393 vs 0..390`, so the geometric branches fired in a recorded run. It is a pinning weakness in a test, not a missing fail direction. I did not re-pin it.
