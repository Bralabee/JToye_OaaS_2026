--- Pre-read finding: can convertAndSend leak a raw JacksonException into the flusher? (no)
javap -c on spring-amqp-4.1.1.jar AbstractJacksonMessageConverter:
  createMessage(Object, MessageProperties, Type): exception table catches java/io/IOException and
  tools/jackson/core/JacksonException over writeValueAsBytes/writeValueAsString, then
  new MessageConversionException(String, Throwable).
  fromMessage(Message, Object): convertContent wrapped the same way (IOException, JacksonException
  -> MessageConversionException).
So in PaymentEventOutboxFlusher.publishRow only objectMapper.readValue can raise a JacksonException.
A catch (JacksonException) ahead of catch (Exception) poisons exactly what the Jackson-2
catch (JsonProcessingException) poisoned; a broker failure still takes the backoff branch.

--- RESEARCH assumption A2: does the Jackson-3 converter read the numeric-timestamp form?
YES, with the DEFAULT converter mapper (new JacksonJsonMessageConverter(String...), which builds
JsonMapper.builder().findAndAddModules(...) with FAIL_ON_UNKNOWN_PROPERTIES and
DEFAULT_VIEW_INCLUSION disabled). amqp/OrderStateChangeEvent.body carries
"timestamp":1791117296.123456789 and converts to GoldenSamples.orderStateChangeEvent() with the
instant equal to the nanosecond; amqp/OrderStateChangeEvent-offset.body (written at +01:00, offset
dropped) converts to an instant equal to GoldenSamples.orderStateChangeEventOffset().
The (JsonMapper, String...) fallback constructor was therefore NOT needed, and no drain step is.
The comparison can fail: the permanent negative controls sameEvent_rejectsAOneNanosecondShift and
sameEvent_rejectsALostComponent pass only because assertSameEvent throws on a 1 ns shift and on a
lost shopId.
