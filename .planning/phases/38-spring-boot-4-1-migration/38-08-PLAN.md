<objective>
Move the messaging path to Jackson 3 and prove that state in flight at deploy time survives it:
messages already on the broker (written by the Boot-3.5 converter) and PENDING rows in the two
transactional outboxes (written by the Boot-3.5 publishers).

Purpose: D-01 names in-flight AMQP messages as a hazard: "prove it, or flush them as a recorded
deploy step". Research found the outbox rows are the same hazard one hop earlier, and they sit in
Postgres for minutes during a broker outage (Phase 27), so they are the likelier victims. Proving
compatibility in code means no operator step is needed.

Output: the eight messaging classes on tools.jackson, three compatibility tests over the 38-01
fixtures (both directions), and a real-broker dispatch test, with trust and mutation arms.
</objective>
