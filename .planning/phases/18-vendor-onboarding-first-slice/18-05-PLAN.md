<objective>
Close the go-live loop: implement the `ALLERGEN_DATA_COMPLETE` gate (computed from the existing V41 product fields — no new product columns), add the guarded `POST /onboarding/go-live` endpoint that publishes the shop, and harden `Shop.published` so the GO_LIVE side effect is the *only* path that can set it true. This is the legal "before publish" gate from the research (Natasha's Law distance-selling allergen duty) plus the vendor-facing go-live action.

Purpose: a vendor who has completed verification and filled in allergen data can take their shop live in one guarded action, and a vendor with incomplete allergen data is blocked from publishing.
Output: `AllergenCompletenessGate`, the go-live endpoint + service method, the sole-writer regression guard, and unit + integration proofs.
</objective>
