## 5. Testcontainers Verification under `postgres:18-alpine` (`NOSUPERUSER NOBYPASSRLS`)

In PostgreSQL 16+ (commit `a0ffa885e`), `ALTER ROLE <role> NOSUPERUSER` is rejected on the `initdb` bootstrap superuser (`oid = 10`: `ERROR: permission denied to alter role / Detail: The bootstrap superuser must have the SUPERUSER attribute`). `IntegrationTestSupport.ensureDowngradableTestRole` renames the `oid = 10` bootstrap role to `jtoye_bootstrap_su` and recreates the Testcontainers test role (`"test"`) as a non-bootstrap `SUPERUSER` (`oid > 10`) before Flyway runs, allowing all 31 integration test classes that execute `ALTER ROLE "<test>" NOSUPERUSER` to downgrade cleanly on PostgreSQL 18.
