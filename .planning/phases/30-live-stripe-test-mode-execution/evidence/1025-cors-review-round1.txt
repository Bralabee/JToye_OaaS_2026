## Step 3: what makes the gate fail the same way on THIS host
Chosen: check-render-invariants.sh runs the scanner under every OTHER awk present (gawk, mawk, nawk, original-awk,
busybox awk; de-duplicated by resolved binary) and makes a failure, or any byte difference from awk's output, a PARSE ERROR.
Why not pin one interpreter: pinning gawk VOIDs on this host (no gawk installed); pinning mawk would still pass the
gawk-only break. The cross-check costs nothing when only one awk exists (it says so) and, here, busybox awk refuses exactly
what gawk refused (old busybox above). A selftest arm (awk-portability) plants the old regex and must exit 2.
