## 0. The question and the ruling

30-20 left "card-only vs Klarna/Link on /pay" with the owner. On 2026-11-01 the
owner ruled: "do Klarna and Link on /pay". Nothing in code restricted the form
to cards: `PaymentService` creates every intent with
`automatic_payment_methods.enabled=true` (lines 230 and 361) and
`card-payment-form.tsx` mounts `<PaymentElement options={{ layout: "tabs" }}>`,
so the Element shows whatever the account's payment-method configuration turns
on. The ruling therefore changes no code; it needed PROOF that the two
redirect/wallet methods complete on /pay, since 30-20 only ever paid with 4242.

## 8. The owning-account column: recommendation for the owner (undecided 2026-11-01)

The question (#1064 round-1 finding 2): the Stripe account an intent lives on
is re-derived from the shop's CURRENT settlement routing on every later call —
`RefundService` (resolveSettlementRouting → Stripe-Account header or
reverse_transfer), `cancelAbandonedPaymentIntent` and the payment-link redeem
replay (`directChargeAccountFor`) — instead of being recorded when the intent
is created. Read from the code 2026-11-01:

- `PaymentService.createPaymentIntent` lines 240-268: with a routing decision the
  intent goes on the connected account (DIRECT, header) or carries
  `transfer_data.destination` (DESTINATION). WITHOUT one (no ENABLED Connect
  account, or no onboarding model) the else branch (255-268) still asks
  `resolveDestinationAccount` and would attach `transfer_data.destination` if it
  answered — but that fallback is dead today: a non-empty answer needs
  MARKETPLACE + ENABLED, which already yields a routing decision. So in practice
  the no-decision intent is a plain PLATFORM intent; the staging card spec
  prints `account=none` for exactly this case, so platform intents are a real
  path, not a theory. V89 must either delete that else branch or stamp
  DESTINATION inside it, never PLATFORM.
- `RefundService` lines 224-241: the refund re-resolves routing and, for DIRECT,
  sends the Stripe-Account header with the stored `pi_`.

So the failing sequence is not only "a settlement-mode change between issue and
redeem" (what round 1 recorded). It is also the ordinary onboarding order of
events: a tenant takes card orders on the platform account, THEN connects
Stripe (DIRECT is the WHITE_LABEL default); from that moment every refund of a
pre-Connect order is sent to the connected account, Stripe answers
`resource_missing` for the `pi_`, and the refund fails. Loud, not silent (the
refund row is marked failed and logged), and no money moves wrongly — but the
vendor can never refund those orders, and nothing in the current tests covers
it (no IT changes routing between intent and refund).

Recommendation: DO IT, as one `/gsd-quick` after Phase 30 closes and before the
first production tenant connects Stripe. WHETHER to do it is the open decision
(HANDOFF.md says so); only the shape below was accepted when the morning's
recommendations were approved. Shape: V89 adds nullable
`orders.payment_settlement_mode` VARCHAR(32) (CHECK on the three
SettlementPolicyMode values — DIRECT_CHARGE_VENDOR_MOR,
DESTINATION_CHARGE_PLATFORM_MOR, SPLIT_DEPOSIT_VENDOR_MOR — plus PLATFORM) and nullable
`orders.payment_stripe_account_id` VARCHAR(255), both mirrored on `orders_aud`
in the same migration (Order is `@Audited`), no backfill and no DEFAULT (NULL =
not recorded, the V63 rule) and therefore no tenant loop; the TWO intent
writers — `PaymentService.createPaymentIntent` (`PaymentIntent.create` at line
274) and `createBalancePaymentIntent` (line 388), which every caller goes
through (the payment-link issue calls them at `OrderPaymentLinkService`
207/209, the storefront at `PublicStorefrontService` 916/1583) — set both at
creation; every reader that re-derives routing uses the recorded pair when
present and falls back to today's derivation when NULL: `RefundService`,
`cancelAbandonedPaymentIntent`, and `retrieveClientSecret(Order, String)`
(lines 412-423) with both of its callers, the payment-link redeem
(`OrderPaymentLinkService` 314/318) and the storefront's V50 idempotent replay
(`PublicStorefrontService` 1197/1206). The redeem and the replay are READERS,
not writers: they retrieve the stored `pi_` on the derived account, so a
pre-Connect DRAFT order's replay would fail the same way a refund does. Proof: an IT that creates a
platform intent, flips the shop to DIRECT with an ENABLED account, and refunds —
RED on the unfixed tree (header sent), GREEN with the column; plus the break arm
that removes the fallback and watches a pre-V89 order's refund fail. Two
columns rather than one, because a NULL account id cannot distinguish "recorded
as platform" from "not recorded", and a DESTINATION intent is also a
platform-account intent whose refund needs `reverse_transfer`, not a header.
