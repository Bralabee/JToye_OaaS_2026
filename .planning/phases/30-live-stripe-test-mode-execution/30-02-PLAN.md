<objective>
Issue #957 (P0), PR 1 part 2 of 4 — the page fails loud. Branch: `fix/957-card-checkout-payment-step` (continues 30-01).

Even with the key channel open, a fork, a local build or a future CI edit can ship an image without a key, and Stripe.js
can fail to load. Today both recreate #957 exactly: the render guard `if (paymentState && stripePromise)` falls through
to the form with no error. This plan makes every failure visible (UI-SPEC § S1): a card shop with no key never POSTs, and
after a POST the payment step always renders — loading, ready, or a stated failure. `PaymentForm` moves out of the
page as `CardPaymentForm` so `/pay` (plan 30-18) reuses it, and the customer order page stops calling an unpaid DRAFT
"Order in Progress".

Incremental Betterment — goods this plan displaces, each accounted for: (1) the #793 lazy load: kept (no Stripe on the
form or on cash checkout; loader only after a clientSecret); (2) stripe-lazy-load.test.tsx property 4 asserted that a
keyless card order POSTs and re-enables Place order — that assertion encoded the #957 defect, so it is REPLACED by
"no POST, alert shown, Place order disabled", and the property it protected (no loadStripe call without a key) is kept;
(3) the step-2 surface (allergen record, SellerBlock, totals, Pay CTA, "Secured by Stripe"): moved unchanged; (4) the
orange #f97316 inside the iframe: replaced by #c2410c, the app's AA shade for the same role (UI-SPEC Color);
(5) "Back to details" key rotation: kept.
Output: the fail-loud checkout, CardPaymentForm, the DRAFT copy, tests, counts.
</objective>
