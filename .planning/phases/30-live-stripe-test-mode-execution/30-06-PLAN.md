Issue #958, PR 2 part 2 of 4 — staff see why an order will not move, and which orders are waiting for money. Branch:
`fix/958-awaiting-payment-gate`.

The kitchen grid swallows the server's refusal (`catch {}` → "Failed to … Reverting."), the New lane's Accept falls through
to "Check your connection" because the re-read still says PENDING, and nothing marks an unpaid order before a cook taps it.

marks the order on the board and in the orders list, and the list stops offering Submit/Confirm for it.

Incremental Betterment — displaced goods: (1) the kitchen's optimistic move + revert: kept (fetchOrders still runs);
(2) the toast title "Error updating order" and generic description: replaced by "Order not updated" + the server's reason,
strictly more informative; (3) the New lane's stock/moved-elsewhere/generic copy: kept, the new branch is first;
(4) Submit/Confirm on the orders list: hidden only for awaiting-payment orders (the server refuses them anyway), Cancel kept.
Output: predicate, badge, three surfaces, tests, counts.
