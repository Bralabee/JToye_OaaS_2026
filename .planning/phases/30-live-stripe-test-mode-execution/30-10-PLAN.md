<objective>
Issue #108, Stripe half, PR 3 part 1 of 2. Branch: `fix/108-stripe-timeouts` (cut in plan 30-08).

No Stripe timeout is set anywhere: stripe-java 34.0.0 defaults to 30 s connect / 80 s read with 2 retries, so one hung call
can hold a checkout request thread for minutes. Refund creation and the webhook's charge lookup are also outside the
`stripe` breaker. 30-RESEARCH §#108 verified the SDK setters and defaults from the sources jar. This plan sets the
timeouts from configuration, puts the two remaining call sites behind breakers of their own (stripe-refund, stripe-lookup),
makes every Stripe breaker count only transport and provider failures (never a 4xx business rejection), and gives an open
breaker a typed 503
instead of a generic 500 (agent-readiness: typed, machine-parseable errors). The SMTP/axios/S3/email-breaker half stays on
#108 (D-10). PaymentIntent create/retrieve and the billing service's public methods are already behind the breaker
(read 2026-10-21: StripeBillingService subscribeOrUpdatePlan, completeWalkSetup, cancelSubscription carry it).
Output: config, two breaker seams on their own instances, the failure predicate, the 503 mapping, tests, counts.
</objective>
