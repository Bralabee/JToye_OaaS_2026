<decisions>

### D-D. The exclusion disclosure is generic, not counted — and that is a strictly weaker claim

33-07's near-you row counts its exclusions (`N kitchens have no location data yet…, and M more are
further than 3.1 miles away`). This plan **cannot** do that: the counts require a second, unfiltered
request, and the standing web-performance criterion forbids adding a round trip to a public route.

So the proximity summary states the exclusion generically and truthfully, with a route out:
> Kitchens we cannot place, and any further away, are not shown. **See every kitchen**.

This is weaker than 33-07's form and is recorded as weaker rather than presented as equivalent. It
still obeys the binding half of the rule: **exclusions are disclosed, never silently dropped, and
nothing is ever computed by subtraction** — 33-07's WR-01 defect was exactly a subtraction over two
page-truncated lists.

*Rejected: a second request to get the unfiltered total.* One extra round trip on the LCP-critical
public route to render a number nobody asked for.

*Rejected: reusing 33-07's arithmetic.* It needs both lists complete and both totals known; here only
one list exists.

### D-A is NOT settled — it is the question the owner gate asks

33-08 chose to run the postcode tier **third**, after FTS. That means `SE15 5BS` is answered as a text
match (the kitchen at that address) rather than as a locality question (every kitchen nearby). It is
defensible and it is reversible in one statement. **Task 3 puts it to the owner explicitly.** Do not
present it as decided.

</decisions>

**1. `frontend/lib/search-interpretation.ts`** — the single parser and the single place a proximity
heading can be produced. Export:

- `export type SearchInterpretation = { kind: "text" } | { kind: "proximity"; postcode: string; precision: "unit" | "district"; radiusKm: number }`
- `parseSearchInterpretation(raw: string | null | undefined): SearchInterpretation` — splits on `;`,
  trims, reads `k=v` pairs. **Anything it cannot parse returns `{kind:"text"}`.** Absence,
  malformation, an unknown kind, a missing `radiusKm`, a non-finite `radiusKm`, an unknown

  claim* proximity, never invent one.
- `formatPostcodeForDisplay(key, precision)` — `district` renders the key as-is (`SE22`); `unit`
  inserts a space before the final three characters (`SE155BS` -> `SE15 5BS`). Formatting only; it
  invents no claim.
- `searchSummary(interpretation, totalElements, query)` returning the summary string, with the
  proximity radius rendered through `formatMiles` from `@/lib/distance` — **never a second literal**.
  `formatMiles(5)` is `"3.1 miles"`, and 33-07 records why it must not be tidied to "3 miles":
  3 miles is 4.83 km, a radius nothing applied.
