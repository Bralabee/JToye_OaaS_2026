# Rubric — what "good" means for J'Toye

Sanmi's past verdicts as checkable lines. A separate scorer (never the builder) checks each run's
screenshots against these and taste-skill's `references/critique.md` at design-run step 8.

- **R1 · Reads as food commerce, not a publication.** A first-time visitor would say "food
  ordering" in five seconds. (PR #49 rejected: "a newspaper outlet, not a food delivery business".)
- **R2 · Directions differ in structure, not skin.** Two candidates with the same skeleton count as
  one. (Sketch 005: all four rejected as re-skins.)
- **R3 · Appetising and enticing, not bland or rigid.** Food is the hero, at a size you can taste;
  the grid can break. (004-D judged "bland, rigid, unenticing", 2026-08-31.)
- **R4 · Laid out on a real grid at every width.** No absolute-vh compositions that fall apart off
  mobile. (006 round 1 was malformed off-mobile and rebuilt on a 1280-capped 12-column grid.)
- **R5 · Navigation is predictable.** The wordmark goes home from everywhere (D6).
- **R6 · Honest.** No claim beyond what the platform does today (`brief.md` constraints).
- **R7 · Phone first, proven on a phone profile.** The 390-wide composition is the primary artifact
  and is judged first; desktop is its enlargement. Hover never latches on touch; the first screen
  carries the headline, one sentence and the search; CWV measured on a throttled mobile profile.
  (Sanmi, 2026-11-01: "majority of users will interact … via phone tablet … ahead of
  laptops/desktops"; the step-3 pick had been recommended from 1280 captures.)
- **R8 · Warm and light, not dark or heavy.** The palette and the hero read as hungry-making and
  friendly; a dark slab or sombre ground fails. (Landing run 2026-11-01: blind A/B, the old page
  beat The Pass build; "too dark / heavy / serious".)
- **R9 · Clear before clever.** In the first screen a visitor knows what to do, as plainly as the old
  page's two doors. (Same A/B: "old one is clearer / friendlier".)
- **R10 · Food all the way down.** Appetite does not stop at the hero: dishes, not street fronts,
  carry the page, and kitchen cards show food. (Same A/B: "food disappears after the hero".)
- **R11 · Elevate what exists before replacing it.** The scope (elevate vs redesign) is the owner's
  call at step 0; the default is elevation: keep the old page's structure and what works, replace
  emoji with real food pictures, remove the tells. (Round 2: "the original landing page needed
  elevation improvements, images rather than emojis, not a complete overhaul".)
- **R12 · Directions come from the owner's references.** Chowdeck, Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat
  are the frame; a direction that cannot name what it takes from them is out. (Round 2: "ignored
  options like chowdeck, ubereats, deliverroo and justeat".)
- **R13 · A palette is a system with a source.** Every colour traces to the brand or a reference, and
  the proposal states its system (one brand hue with a tint/shade ramp, neutrals, one accent, the
  60-30-10 split, contrast). No colour "from nowhere". (Round 2: "the red colour from out of nowhere
  is cringe"; "not … cognisant of colour theories".)
- **R14 · The phone's first screen shows food at a size you can taste.** Chip thumbnails do not
  count. (Elevate run 2026-11-01: the build won the blind A/B, and the scorer's top weakness was
  the phone first screen with no real food picture.)
