## What they agree on (input to step 3)

1. Photograph of food or a place, full-bleed, with type set on a solid block or on the photo.
2. One control on the first screen that starts an order (an address or search field + one button).
3. Secondary personas as doors BELOW the customer's screen, each with its own picture.
4. One typeface family doing display and body.
5. Nothing that could be mistaken for a template: no gradient wash, no glass, no icon-in-circle
   feature grid on the first screen.
6. Where the big UK apps show a stock burger or bowl, J'Toye's picture is a particular kitchen's
   world: a Rye Lane or Brixton shopfront, jollof, suya, jerk (D10).
