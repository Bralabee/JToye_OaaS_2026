# Identity — J'Toye public surfaces

**Status: RULED by Sanmi 2026-11-01 (design-run step 4 of the landing run, from the picked direction The
Pass; decision D11).** Every later change is logged in `decisions.md`.

## Palette (six named colours, flat; no gradients, D9)

| role | name | hex | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| ground | stone | `#F2F1EE` | the page |
| ground step | deeper stone | `#E3DED6` | a surface a tone down; replaces every rule and shadow |
| paper | ticket white | `#FFFFFF` | the order ticket, the search field, cards that are controls |
| brand | oxblood | `#3A0B0D` | the slab, the footer, headings; the parent-site thread kept on its merits (D4) |
| brand deep | oxblood deep | `#1C0405` | type on amber; the darkest field |
| action | amber | `#F0A322` | the one accent: the action and the active state, nothing else |

Ink `#1F1715` on stone; cream `#F7F7F5` on oxblood. Status words keep their own semantic colours
(open `#15724A`, paused `#D98A12`, closed `#8A8078`) and are never the accent. Food photography supplies
every other colour. Not used: the cream-and-orange wash (the assistant palette), violet, orange-700 as a
button fill (reads as terracotta beside oxblood).

## Type (one family)

**Work Sans** does everything. Display 800, tracking -0.03em, leading 0.95, `text-wrap: balance`;
text 400 with 500 for emphasis, 17px body, leading 1.55, tabular figures. Five steps on a page:
small 15px · body 17px · lead 20px · heading `clamp(1.75rem, 4.6vw, 3.6rem)` · the hero and the footer
name larger. Corners decided once: square, the plate's circle excepted. Buttons: flat amber with
oxblood-deep type, square, 52px tall; hover inverts to oxblood with cream type.

## Voice

Plain, in the kitchen's own words, and only what the platform does today (rubric R6). Headlines are
titles of four to six words ("Order from kitchens near you"). No slogans, no "delicious", no "seamless",
no exclamation marks, no claims of scale. The food is named (jollof, suya, jerk), the street is named
(Rye Lane, Brixton Market), the facts are the three true ones (hygiene rating verified, allergens on
every item, nothing to download).

## The one memorable device

**The order ticket on the pass.** A white ticket with a perforated top edge (an SVG circle tile, never a
gradient) carries each step, each kitchen row, the operator's kitchen display. The hero's plate breaks
the oxblood slab's diagonal edge, and the headline sits over the plate's edge. One motion moment per
page at most: the plate settling on load, CSS only, reduced-motion still.

## Imagery (D10)

Real, credited pictures of the kitchens' world until each kitchen's own photos exist; the four real
product screens for operators; never a stock burger, never an emoji, never a drawn dish.
